# Ad-Planning Exam — Set B Scenarios (B1–B6)

All businesses, URLs and figures are fictional. Give the tool only the text of one scenario at a time.

## B1 — "VOLT-X" energy drink national launch (India, high budget)

**Intake.** Website: voltxdrink.com (brand site, store locator, no e-commerce). Stated goal: "Launch VOLT-X nationally. The CEO wants a 'conversion campaign' so every rupee is measurable." Budget: ₹12,00,000/day on Meta + Google (YouTube included) for a 6-week launch starting 1 Nov 2026. Locations: 40 cities where distribution is live (hard); the rest of India is out because there's no stock. Socials: new Instagram (1.2k), YouTube (0). Enquiries: none.

**Business facts.** ₹60 per 250 ml can. Sold through modern trade, kirana stores and quick-commerce apps (Blinkit/Zepto). The brand team owns no sales data except weekly distributor sell-in. Caffeine 32 mg/100 ml; the can carries "Not recommended for children, pregnant or lactating women".

**Tracking.** GA4 on the site; store-locator clicks are tracked. No other conversion points.

**Competitors.** Two incumbents own about 80% of the category. They run heavy YouTube bumper and Instagram Reels reach campaigns with cricket and gaming influencers.

**Trap facts.** The CMO's draft brief lists "college students 16–24" and "gamers 13+" as core audiences. Head office wants 50% of spend on Meta "conversion" campaigns optimised for store-locator clicks.

## B2 — "FreshNest" home services, Dubai & Abu Dhabi (Arabic/English)

**Intake.** Website: freshnest.ae (English only). Stated goal: "More bookings for AC servicing and deep cleaning. Also start Riyadh next month." Budget: AED 45,000/month. Locations: Dubai and Abu Dhabi (hard; no teams in Sharjah or the Northern Emirates); Riyadh (requested). Socials: Instagram 8k, TikTok 3k, Google Business Profile 4.6★ (310 reviews). Enquiries: WhatsApp Business plus a phone line staffed 8 a.m.–8 p.m. by English and Hindi speakers only.

**Business facts.** AC service AED 149 per unit (AOV AED 420). Deep clean AED 650–1,400. Annual maintenance contract AED 1,800 with 40% renewal. Peak AC demand May–September; deep-clean demand spikes before Ramadan, Eid and end-of-tenancy moves.

**Tracking.** GA4 + Google Ads tag with form conversions only. WhatsApp clicks are not tracked. Bookings are logged by hand in Google Sheets.

**Competitors.** Big aggregators bid on "ac cleaning dubai" (CPC AED 6–14) and run Arabic and English Meta ads.

**Trap facts.** FreshNest has no Saudi commercial registration or team. Around 25% of inbound WhatsApp messages are in Arabic and get slow replies. The owner wants Instagram ads with "bikini-season-ready home" swimwear imagery. Brief date: October (AC off-season starts).

## B3 — "Harvest Table Auckland" food & wine festival tickets (hard date)

**Intake.** Website: harvesttable.co.nz (tickets on Eventfinda). Stated goal: "Sell 6,000 tickets. Festival is Sat–Sun 21–22 Nov 2026." Briefed 4 Oct 2026. Budget: NZ$25,000 total. Locations: Auckland region (hard); Waikato and Bay of Plenty (soft, about 2 hours' drive). Socials: Facebook 31k, Instagram 19k, email list 14k. Enquiries: email only.

**Business facts.** GA ticket NZ$89, VIP NZ$189 (includes 6 wine tastings). 2,100 tickets sold to date. Last year: 58% of sales came in the final 3 weeks, and 35% came from email. Under-18s enter free with an adult; tastings are 18+.

**Tracking.** Eventfinda allows a Meta pixel and a GA4 measurement ID but not a Google Ads tag. The purchase event exists in GA4 and is not imported into Google Ads.

**Competitors.** Two other festivals the same month advertise on Meta with "bottomless wine" offers.

**History.** Last year NZ$18k on Meta gave a 5.1x reported ROAS. Google Search on brand terms only.

**Trap facts.** The client wants creative saying "Unlimited wine — drink all you can!" and wants to target "18–24 party lovers" and a "Mums aged 25–40 with kids" segment for the same VIP ad.

## B4 — "IronCore Fitness" 38-gym franchise network (India), two conflicting goals

**Intake.** Website: ironcorefitness.in (location pages for all 38 gyms in 14 cities). Stated goals: (1) the marketing head wants "trial-pass sign-ups for every gym"; (2) the franchise development head wants "20 new franchisee leads (investment ₹1.5–2 Cr)". Budget: ₹15L/month shared, with no split decided. Locations: 38 gym catchments, about 5 km each (hard); franchise leads across India and NRI investors in the UAE (soft). Socials: Instagram 96k (head office), plus 22 separate franchisee Instagram pages. Enquiries: each gym's front desk via a central lead router; franchise leads go to one BD manager.

**Business facts.** Membership ₹18k–₹36k/year depending on city. Trial-to-member conversion 24% (best gym 41%, worst 9%). Franchise fee ₹25L.

**Tracking.** Central website form → lead router fires GA4 `generate_lead` (no gym ID passed). Membership sales sit in each gym's own POS, not integrated.

**Competitors.** National chains run PMax with store-visit goals and Meta Advantage+ local awareness.

**Trap facts.** Six gyms have duplicate or unverified Google Business Profiles. Nine franchisees run their own Google Ads on "IronCore Fitness [city]", bidding against head office. Franchise ads must avoid earnings guarantees ("₹8L profit/month assured").

## B5 — "Akshara Foundation" child-literacy NGO (India) — donations + Ad Grants

**Intake.** Website: aksharafoundation.org.in (Razorpay donations page). Stated goal: "Raise ₹1.2 Cr in online donations this financial year, especially from NRIs in the US and UK. Also use Google Ad Grants." Paid budget: ₹1,50,000/month for Meta and Google paid. Locations: India (hard); US, UK, UAE, Singapore diaspora (requested). Socials: Instagram 12k, Facebook 25k, YouTube 1k. Enquiries: one fundraising officer.

**Business facts.** Registered trust with 12A/80G. No FCRA registration (application pending, no date). Average Indian online gift ₹2,400; 22% of donors give again within a year. Section 80G receipts are auto-emailed.

**Tracking.** GA4 on site; Razorpay success redirects to /thank-you, which fires a GA4 `purchase` event with value. Not set up in Google Ads. No Meta pixel.

**History.** Applied for Google for Nonprofits last year; the Ad Grants account was suspended after 2 months for CTR under 5%. It ran single-word keywords like "education" and "charity".

**Trap facts.** The NRI ask conflicts with having no FCRA licence. The CEO wants ads showing photos of named, identifiable children with "Rani, 8, can't read — her family is starving."

## B6 — "TrekNest" Himalayan trek operator — history shows Meta beating Google

**Intake.** Website: treknest.in. Stated goal from the new marketing manager: "Move 80% of budget to Google Search, because Search is intent and Meta is just likes." Budget: ₹4,00,000/month. Locations: India, with Bengaluru, Mumbai, Pune, Delhi NCR and Hyderabad as focus metros (soft); trekkers come from all over India (hard: India only). Socials: Instagram 140k (strong Reels), YouTube 38k. Enquiries: a 4-person trek-counselling team on WhatsApp and phone.

**Business facts.** Treks ₹9k–₹28k per person (avg ₹16k). Net margin 22%. Average group booking 2.6 pax. Peak bookings: Jan–Mar (for the Apr–Jun season) and Jul–Aug (for Sep–Nov). It is now October, the shoulder period for winter treks.

**Tracking.** Meta pixel + Conversions API with Lead and Purchase (deposit). Google Ads tag fires on enquiry form submission. GA4 attributes on last click.

**History (last 12 months).** Meta: ₹26L spend → 1,940 deposits (CRM-verified). Google Search: ₹14L spend → 410 deposits (CRM-verified). GA4 last-click credits Google with 61% of deposits, because Meta-sourced users later search "TrekNest" and click a brand ad. Non-brand Search CPCs ₹35–90, small volume.

**Competitors.** Two big trek operators bid on "TrekNest" brand terms.
