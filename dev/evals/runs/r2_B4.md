# B4 IronCore Fitness (live checks of GTM, GBP, Ad Library and policy were NOT done; flagged "verify live")

## Outcome and economics
- Real outcome (gyms): paid annual member from an attended trial. Margin not given, so I assumed 50%: avg ₹27k x 50% = ₹13.5k break-even per member; at 24% trial-to-member (assumed to mean attended trial) = ₹3,240 per attended trial. Lead to attended is assumed 25%, so break-even is about ₹800 per lead. Target ≤₹6k per member.
- Real outcome (franchise): signed franchisee (fee ₹25L; assumed 40% contribution = ₹10L). Target ≤₹25k per qualified investor lead (assumed 1 in 15 signs).
- Transaction: gym front desk and POS (walk-in), and a BD meeting for franchise. Platforms see only the form lead. Reconcile via a gym ID in the router, front-desk trial codes, a weekly POS tally per gym, then offline import/CAPI.

## Goal translation
- Marketing head: "trial passes for every gym" becomes attended trials to paid members; all 38 gyms targeted, money follows gym count and front-desk conversion; 9%-conversion gyms get a process fix first.
- Franchise head: "20 leads" becomes 20 qualified investors (₹1.5–2Cr capacity, ≤6 months), capped at 18% (₹9k/day), franchise head signs off.
- Declined: "₹8L profit/month assured" (misleading). Alternative: state the investment range, fee and support, plus "illustrative model, not a guarantee".

## Platform split (₹15L/mo = ₹50k/day, joint pool)
Franchise ₹9k. Consumer ₹41k: Meta ₹22.5k, Search ₹17k, brand ₹1.5k. Search is 43% of non-brand, 12 points below the 55% default. Reason: derived cost per member is ₹4.2k on Meta versus ₹5.9k on Search, and Meta needs the budget. Brand is funded first (franchisees bid on it).

## Operating constraints
- Lead router must pass gym_id. First reply within 30 minutes, with an away message after hours and a next-morning follow-up.
- Off-peak trial slots (11am–4pm) as the offer where gyms are empty. Pause pins when a gym is at capacity.
- Franchise: one BD manager handles ~60–80 screened leads/month. If raw leads exceed 80, cut C4.
- Diwali (Nov) CPM +20–40%. January peak, so build data now.

## Risks and blockers
No gym ID/POS link (no spend until gym_id and trial codes live); franchisees bidding on brand (must stop; shared negatives); 6 broken GBPs (no location assets there); Pixel/CAPI unknown; front-desk speed; assumed margin.
