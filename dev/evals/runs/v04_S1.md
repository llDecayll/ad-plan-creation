# S1 — SmileCraft Dental — Paid Ads Plan (Oct 2026) — run v04
Overall **69/100: launch with the listed fixes.** All costs come from the plugin's benchmark table. Nothing was checked live and there is no history.

## Real outcome, economics, where the transaction happens
- **Real outcome:** a new patient who books **and attends**, ideally on a weekday. Implants and aligners are the most valuable.
- **Economics (assumed, client to confirm):** ~₹10-15k first-year revenue per patient at ~40% margin. Break-even is about **₹4-6k per attended patient**; the target is **≤₹2,000**.
- **Where it happens:** /book form (Pixel+CAPI Lead and Google conversion both verified), WhatsApp (counted by Meta) and calls. The visit happens in the clinic, which the platforms can't see.
- **Reconciliation:** reception keeps a daily tally of booked, attended, treatment and source. Source comes from the campaign code in the WhatsApp greeting and from UTMs/gclid in hidden /book fields. Reconcile weekly.

## Goal translation
- The client asked for "awareness", but the owner says chairs are empty on weekdays. That is a **bookings** problem, so I recommend **Leads objectives, not Awareness**.
- Search and Meta delivery in both areas give local visibility anyway. A reach campaign is deferred (see below).
- No ask breaks policy, so nothing is declined. I avoid a "₹999 cleaning" price hook because it brings low-value leads.

## Platform split: Google 60% / Meta 40%
- With no history, the plugin's intent order applies: Search first for local services people search for (its range is 55-70%).
- Search also catches implant and aligner intent.
- Forecast cost per attended patient is similar on both platforms. The larger Google share is driven by intent and patient value, not cost (stated constraint).
- Re-split on day 30 by real cost per attended patient.

## Deferred (client to decide) and other channels
- **Meta reach:** (80k ÷ 30 days) × freq 4 × ₹150 CPM ÷ 1,000 ≈ **₹1,600/day**. Trigger: weekday chairs are full.
- **Performance Max:** needs ≥1.5 × CPA ≈ **₹1,125/day** and ≥30 conversions/month.
- **Brand Search:** **₹150-300/day** if competitors bid on the brand.
- **Retargeting:** the pool is too small; revisit at 60 days.
- **Other channels (outside this plugin):** Google Business Profile (GBP) posts and booking link (the 4.8★/310-review profile, free), Practo/Justdial (~₹5-10k/month), and a WhatsApp broadcast to existing patients offering weekday recall slots.

## Special ad category and policy
- Meta special ad category: **none**. The topic isn't housing, employment or credit, and the audience is in India. The securities declaration doesn't apply.
- Health rules: no copy implying the viewer has a condition, no guarantees, no before/after on Meta.
- The ₹500 free X-ray offer must be honoured.
- Live policy checks were skipped (no browsing allowed).

## Operating constraints applied
- Reception works 9am-8pm and replies in about 10 minutes.
- **Google:** call asset scheduled 9am-8pm; the form runs 24h.
- **Meta:** daily budget plus an away message from 8pm to 9am with the /book link; the queue is cleared by 9:30am.
- **Weekday capacity:** creatives and quick replies push weekday slots; kids' dentistry goes in after-school weekday slots.
- **First-reply script:** treatment → preferred weekday/time → book. No questions about symptoms.

## Key risks and blockers
1. Show-up tracking is manual. Without reception's tally, real-outcome measurement fails.
2. M1 becomes learning-limited if CPR is at the high end.
3. The neighbourhood audience is small, so frequency will rise. Refresh creatives every 2-3 weeks.
4. Health-event restrictions may block CRM or offline uploads.
5. The Dental Council of India advertising code is unverified.

- **Location type:** meta-ads-planner step 7 says hard boundary means "People living in", but the intake table says clinics use "living in or recently in". I followed the table.
- **Economics:** break-even is required, but the intake never asks for margin, LTV or close rate. I assumed them.
- **Funnel rates:** no lead→booked→attended benchmarks exist, so attended-patient forecasts are improvised.
- **Dental advertising law:** no Dental Council of India advertising rules. Dental before/after ("body ideal") rules are ambiguous.
- **Health data:** CAPI/offline CRM-stage advice ignores health-data restrictions and DPDP.
- **Benchmark width:** the CPC range ₹20-120 is too wide; the ₹60 midpoint is a judgement call.
- **Tracking factor:** doesn't fit WhatsApp (native event plus a verified pixel elsewhere).
- **Competitor factor:** defined only for the Meta Ad Library.
- **Undefined terms:** "main campaign" is undefined. The forecast cap of 60 sits exactly on a band edge (±35% vs ±50%).
- **Meta scheduling:** lifetime-budget scheduling vs away message has no tie-break rule.
- **HTML output:** the plugin's default output is HTML; skipped per runner instructions.
