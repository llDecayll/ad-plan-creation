# C5 Kaveri Precision: ad plan (USD, $5,000/month joint pool)
No live checks done; all policy and market items are "verify live".

## Real outcome and economics
- Real outcome: a qualified RFQ (named company, drawing, volume at or above the 500 MOQ) that becomes an order.
- Gross profit per order is $30k x 24% = $7,200. At 12% RFQ-to-order, break-even is **$864 per qualified RFQ**; target is $700 or less. Repeat-order value is unknown.
- Sales' "15% convert" is contradicted by show data (6 RFQs and 1 order from 400 leads); I use measured rates.
- **The transaction happens offline** (sales-led quote, 6-9 month cycle). The platforms see only the form, about 30% of enquiries. Reconcile with a free CRM with gclid/UTM fields, campaign codes in mailto and WhatsApp, a weekly tally and monthly offline import.

## Goal translation and declined asks
- "10 RFQs/month" is read as qualified RFQs. At $4.5k the central estimate is about 3.6 (rough range 1.5-10.8); ten needs roughly $12k or more. Raw enquiries (about 18) are plausible. The client must say which.
- **Declined: artillery shell casings.** Ammunition is prohibited on Google and Meta, and export licensing applies. Shell and ordnance terms become negatives. Alternative: advertise industrial machined and cast parts, and pursue defence by direct outreach. Sign-off: MD plus export-compliance adviser.
- **Declined: Russia and Belarus.** Sanctions risk, platform restrictions, and a clash with the client's own hard geography. Alternative: a counsel-cleared UK, Canada, Italy or Poland test. Sign-off: MD plus counsel.
- **Declined: uploading the 400 trade-show leads** (EU contacts have no opt-in; GDPR). The pool is also too small. Alternative: a consent-request email and sales follow-up, then a Custom Audience from documented-consent contacts only. Sign-off: MD and export sales manager.

## Platform split
**Google 100%** ($4,500 funded plus a $500 reserve), **Meta 0%**. Search holds the intent and the client's CPC data. B2B Meta prospecting needs about $300-900 per day for its 3x learning minimum (derived CPR $100-300), above the test cap. There is no retargeting pool (about 640 clicks a month).

## Campaign list
**C1: Google Search, non-brand RFQ, USA + UAE (English).** Lower funnel.
- **Objective and conversion location:** Leads, to a website RFQ page (drawing upload, quantity, country, timeline). Lead form asset off. mailto and WhatsApp clicks tracked as secondary.
- **Bid:** Max clicks with a $9 CPC cap for weeks 1-3, then Max conversions once there are 15 or more tracked conversions in 30 days.
- **Location:** Presence only. AI Max off. **Daily budget:** $148.
- **Keyword themes:** investment casting supplier, CNC machining services, China-plus-one sourcing, machined aluminium, RFQ terms.
- **Negatives:** jobs, course, student, free, DIY, prototype, ammunition, shell, ordnance.
- **Budget check:** $148 x 30.4 = $4,499. Clicks per day are 29.6 at $5, 21.1 at $7 and 16.4 at $9, all at or above 15. The 1x CPA gate fails ($148 is 0.59 x a $250 CPA), so this is a **learning-limited test**.
- **Forecast** (client CPC, assumed 2-4% conversion rate, 15-30% qualified):

| | Low | Mid | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leads | 10 | 18 | 36 |
| Qualified RFQs | 1.5 | 3.6 | 10.8 |
| Cost per lead | $450 | $250 | $125 |
| Cost per qualified RFQ | $3,000 | $1,250 | $417 |

- At mid, 3.6 x 12% = 0.43 orders per month, about $3.1k gross profit against $4.5k spend: **below break-even**.
- **Reserve ($500):** released at day 21 to the best ad group if impression share lost to budget exceeds 20%.

## Deferred and Other channels
- **Germany Search** (German RSAs, consent mode v2): $105 per day, about $3.2k per month. Trigger: C1 cost per qualified RFQ at or below $1,300 at day 30. Client decides Germany-first versus US/UAE-first.
- **Meta retargeting or prospecting:** $300-900 per day. Trigger: a 30-day site pool of at least 3,000.
- **Other channels:** LinkedIn by job title (30-50% of a B2B budget), Thomasnet, Wer liefert was, IndiaMART, consent-based email nurture.

## Policy and operating constraints
- **Special ad category:** none (industrial B2B). Not required, do not opt in. Consent mode v2 needed for Germany. Check UAE permits.
- **Operations:** one sales manager in IST. No call asset (US overnight). Always-on ads, an auto-acknowledge email and a booking link, with callback windows of 18:00-20:00 IST. First reply within 1 business day.

## Key risks and blockers
Tracking blind to 70% of enquiries. Weak RFQ page. Forecast below break-even. Goal unrealistic at this budget. Ammunition content on the domain.

## Confidence
- **C1: 43/100 now**, about 56-60 after fixes. **Overall is also 43**, since C1 is the only funded campaign.
- **Factors (0-10):** tracking 4, budget 3, cost evidence 6, offer 3, competitors 4, policy 5, seasonality 6. No cap binds. A cap of 55 applies if ammunition stays reachable from the ad domain.
- **Raised by:** verified form tag plus CRM tally (+8), live Ad Library scan (+4).
- **Deferred scores** (Germany about 38, Meta about 30) are not averaged.

## Five creative angles for C1
1. **Pain:** late shipments or rejected batches from the current supplier?
2. **Proof:** CMM reports, ISO and a real US$22k order (client to confirm).
3. **Offer:** send a drawing, get a costed quote in 48 hours.
4. **Authority:** drawing-to-first-article process.
5. **Objection-killer:** MOQ 500, landed-cost quote, China-plus-one supply.

## Improvised where the plugin was missing or unclear
1. No bidding rule for tracking that exists but a budget below 1x CPA. I borrowed the Max-clicks row.
2. No scoring guidance for "click gate passes, CPA gate fails".
3. No sanctions, export-control, ammunition or GDPR list-upload rules. I used general knowledge.
4. No rule for a central forecast below break-even. I used a 90-day test with a stop-loss.
5. No B2B manufacturing benchmark for US/DE. I used the client's CPC plus India conversion rates.
6. The playbook says LinkedIn first for B2B, but the pool is Meta+Google. I assumed a joint pool.
7. The market-merge rule is ambiguous when time zone and CPC differ (I merged US and UAE with bid adjustments).
