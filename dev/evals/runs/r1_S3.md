# S3 PeopleOS: plan (run r1)

Assumptions: the ₹8,000/day is one Meta+Google pool (scenario silent); LinkedIn is separate. No live checks done (SERP, Ad Library, Transparency, Trends, policy, brand bidding, PageSpeed): all figures are scenario or playbook data.

## Real outcome, economics, transaction
- Real outcome: **qualified demo, then won deal** (ACV ₹6-12L, cycle 45-90 days). History: 140 demos x ₹4,200 = ₹5.88L; 18% qualified = 25; **₹23.3k per qualified demo**.
- Break-even (win rate 20%, margin 75% **assumed, client to confirm**): ₹9L x 0.2 x 0.75 = **₹1.35L per qualified demo**. Actual is 17% of that, so absorbable demand, not money, is the constraint. Client numbers sit inside benchmark ranges.
- Transaction: demo form on own site (tracked), then sales call and deal in HubSpot (offline). Reconcile with gclid/UTM hidden fields and offline stage import.

## Goal translation
"Demo bookings" stays the optimisation event (volume) but is judged on qualified demos and pipeline. No client asks to decline were stated.

## Platform split
**Google 100% (₹8,000), Meta ₹0.** Google holds our only real cost per outcome. B2B Meta lacks job-title targeting, has no pixel and no retargeting pool, and its CPR (₹500-3,000+) makes a learning unit ₹9-21k/day. A second smart-bidding unit needs at least ₹4,200, so the pool supports one proper campaign plus a small brand one.

## Deferred and other channels
- **UAE Search**: derived CPD ₹12.6-25k (3-6x India, cost evidence ≤3). Needs **₹19k/day minimum, ₹38k comfortable**. Trigger: WPS/gratuity fit, permit check, budget. Not merged with India.
- **Meta retargeting**: needs pixel+CAPI, pool of a few thousand, ~₹9k/day test (3x ₹3k derived CPR).
- **Other channels (outside plugin):** **LinkedIn** lead-gen/ABM (usually 30-50% of B2B; CPL ₹1,500-5,000), suggest separate ₹2.5-3k/day test; software directories (G2, Capterra); email/WhatsApp to own list.

## Operating constraints
Async form, so always-on; reply within 1 business hour; SDR capacity check (54 demos/month is ~2.5 per working day; cap spend if exceeded). Form: work email, company size, role; under-50 tagged low priority. Hard boundary excludes Chennai/Kolkata: flag.
