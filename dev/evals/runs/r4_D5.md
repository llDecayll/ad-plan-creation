# D5 Lingomart plan (USD; no live checks, verify live)

## Real outcome and economics
- Outcome: net take per paying buyer (CFO is right). Net per pack = 110x25% - 3% fee = $24.20; x3.1 packs = $75.02 (horizon assumed ~12 months, ask). Trial loses $4.15 (tutor $9 - $5 + $0.15 fee).
- Per trial booked: 0.38 x 75.02 - 4.15 = **$24.36 break-even; 3:1 allowable $8.12**. Per paying buyer: $64.10 break-even; 3:1 $21.37. First-pack-only cash value is $5.05 per trial, so payback is slow.
- Sanity: history $12k / 520 trials = **$23.08 per trial, $60.73 per buyer** (197.6 buyers) = ~95% of break-even, 2.8x the 3:1 allowable. **Verdict: break-even, not profitable at 3:1, so staged start.** History signup-to-trial is 12.7% vs the stated 14%; plan uses 12.7%.
- Transaction: web signup (tagged), apps 60% (install tool only), trial and pack happen in-product. Platforms cannot see trial_booked or pack_paid; reconcile weekly by campaign code and UTM from the backend.

## Goal translation and declined asks
- "10,000 signups at $30k" = $3.00 per signup vs $3.09 break-even (12.7%): zero margin. Replaced by cost per paying buyer, optimised via trial_booked and value (net take) events.
- Declined (sign-off CMO): "Fluent in 30 days, guaranteed"; use "Book a $5 trial lesson with a native tutor". "50,000+ learners" is untrue; use "31,000+ registered" or "9,800 active in 90 days".
- Declined (sign-off CEO plus legal/DPO, not the partner): ads to 13-17s. Alternative: parents 25-54 (lessons for your child) and adults 18+.
- Honoured: CFO net-take goal.
