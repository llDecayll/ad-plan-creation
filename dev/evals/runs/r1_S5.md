# S5 Crumb & Co plan (r1)

**Assumptions (no live checks; plugin would check Ad Library, SERP, GBP, policy live):** ₹300/day is one pool; hours, repliers, delivery radius, margin, capacity unknown.

## Real outcome and economics
- Outcome: **paid custom-cake orders via WhatsApp** (₹1,200-3,000, midpoint AOV ~₹2,100). Not followers.
- Margin not supplied. Labelled assumption (separate from facts): 50% contribution gives break-even ~₹1,050 per order and break-even ROAS 2.0x. Cost evidence capped at 50.
- Transaction: Swiggy/Zomato orders are invisible to Meta/Google. WhatsApp custom orders are countable (conversation native, orders by manual tally with campaign code in the greeting).

## Goal translation
- "More followers": **declined as the goal**. Alternative: a support spend capped at 10% (₹30/day).
- "More online orders": split. Custom cakes go to WhatsApp (Meta). Aggregator orders cannot be tracked; Meta traffic there is weak, so Swiggy/Zomato in-app ads are an Other channel.

## Platform split
Meta ₹300 (100%), Google ₹0.
- Why: no website or tracking, so Search has no landing page or conversion. Search also needs 15 clicks/day x ₹15-80 CPC = ₹225-1,200/day, beyond ₹300.
- Matches the bakery/no-website playbook (Meta + WhatsApp, GBP free).

Festive CPM +20-40% in Oct-Dec (Diwali is early November; verify).

## Five creative angles (C1)
1. Pain: "Birthday tomorrow, no cake?" with 24-48h custom slots (lead time client to confirm).
2. Proof: 9:16 time-lapse of a custom cake build plus a real customer photo and review.
3. Offer: free delivery within 3 km or a free design sketch, festive slots limited (client to confirm).
4. Authority: the baker on camera showing 3 steps (message, pick design, collect), with FSSAI number.
5. Objection-killer: "Custom cakes from ₹1,200", transparent per-kg prices, eggless option if true.
