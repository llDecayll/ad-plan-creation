# A5 RupeeRaft ad plan (run r1)
Live checks skipped (plugin would do them): Ad Library filter, Transparency Center, SERP, policy search, AppsFlyer-to-Meta postback, RBI rules. Everything is baseline/benchmark.

## Real outcome and economics
- Outcome: a **disbursed loan with acceptable repayment risk**. Net revenue ₹1,800 per disbursal: break-even ≤₹1,800, target ≤₹1,200 (my assumption).
- Transaction: Android app, partner NBFC underwrites and disburses. Meta sees installs and AppsFlyer events, not repayment. Reconcile weekly with NBFC disbursal and first-EMI data by campaign code.
- Derived funnel: CPI ₹38 (client) / install-to-KYC 10-35% (Apps benchmark) = ₹109-380 per KYC, mid ₹190. KYC-to-disbursal = 22% approval x 70% take-up (assumed) = 15.4%. Cost per disbursal: **₹708 / ₹1,235 / ₹2,470**. Break-even needs install-to-KYC ≈13.7%, so economics are thin.

## Goal translation and declined asks
- "Disbursals, not applications" is right: optimise KYC-complete, feed disbursal back as value.
- **Declined:** "0% interest" (APR 18-36% + 2-4% fee), "no CIBIL check", "guaranteed approval" (22% approval), and targeting low credit scores (financial vulnerability).
- **Alternatives:** "Check eligibility in 2 minutes", APR/fee/tenure upfront, named RBI-regulated NBFC partner, "subject to eligibility". Target by intent with broad Advantage+. Competitors' claims are not a reason to copy.

## Plugin gaps and improvisation
- **Blocked platform vs absorption:** "park, don't reroute" conflicts with "give the best channel all it absorbs." I chose 67.5/32.5 with an explicit client decision.
- **Event conflict:** Meta skill says deepest event with ≥50/week, Lending note says KYC-complete. I followed Lending and tested disbursal in M2.
- **No lending benchmarks:** I borrowed Apps rows and invented the 70% take-up.
- **Policy gaps:** no-opt-in (policy-watch) vs "always state" (Lending). Nothing on Meta India verification or the RBI app directory; I used my own knowledge.
- **Suspended Google account:** only "appeal or fix", no process.
- **No Meta app-campaign minimum;** I reused 7x CPR.
- **Credit risk:** "acceptable risk" has no forecast or confidence guidance.
- **State exclusions under "living in"** are not covered; the NE list is undefined.
- **Word cap vs five creatives per campaign:** followed the gate's one-line allowance.
