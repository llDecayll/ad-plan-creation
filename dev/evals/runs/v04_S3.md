# S3 — PeopleOS — Paid Ads Plan (v04)
Assumptions: ₹8,000/day is a joint budget; an SDR team answers in IST office hours; qualified→won rate assumed 15-30% (client to confirm). Not checked live: GTM, Ad Library, Transparency Center, Auction Insights, Trends, policy.

## Real outcome and economics
- **Real outcome:** a qualified demo (HubSpot SQL), then closed-won. ACV is ₹6-12L.
- **History:** ₹4,200 per demo ÷ 18% qualified = **₹23.3k per qualified demo**. Last year's spend was ~₹5.9L (~₹1,600/day). This plan is ~4.7× that, so expect a higher marginal CPA.
- **Ceiling (assumed rule):** ad cost per won deal ≤ ⅓ of first-year ACV, so ≤ ₹2-4L. At a 20% close rate: ≤ ₹40k per qualified demo, or **≤ ₹7,200 per demo**.
- **Where it converts:** the demo form on peopleos.io (GA4 + Google Ads conversion, verified). Stages and the contract happen in HubSpot.
- **Reconciliation:** gclid offline import of SQL/Won, enhanced conversions, weekly HubSpot check.

## Goal translation
- "Demo bookings" becomes: optimise for demo submits; judge on cost per qualified demo and pipeline.
- Later, add value rules (demo 1, SQL 5, Won scaled to ACV).
- No client asks needed declining.

## Platform split: Google 100%
- Search is proven and tracked.
- Meta has no pixel and can't target HR heads/CFOs reliably.
- Any Meta unit needs 7 × ₹1-2k CPR = ₹7-14k/day, which the budget can't cover.

## Key risks / blockers

2. Only ~8 SQLs/month, too few to bid on directly; value rules are needed.
3. The close rate is unknown.
4. Whether competitors bid on the brand is unverified.

## Where I improvised
- Intake doesn't say whether the budget is joint or per-platform; I assumed joint.
- The B2B playbook says "LinkedIn first", but the plugin can't plan LinkedIn and gives no rule on whether to carve it out of the stated budget.
- There's no SaaS/ACV break-even formula or default close rate. I invented the ⅓-ACV ceiling and the 15-30% close rate.
- Budget rules cover only smart-bidding units, with nothing for brand or target-impression-share campaigns. The "competitors bid on brand" check can't be verified offline.
- The quality gate wants ≥5 creatives even for the small brand campaign, which is overkill; I gave five for C1 only.
- The day-14 "real outcome" check doesn't fit a 45-90 day cycle. I used qualified demo as the proxy and added a 60-90 day row.
- The retargeting pool rule ("~1,000+ people per day of budget") is ambiguous.
- UAE costs come only from a 3-6× multiplier.
- There's no hard-boundary guidance for B2B buyers who commute across city lines (NCR).
