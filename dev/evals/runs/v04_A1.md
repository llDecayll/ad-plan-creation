# Shaant — Paid Ads Plan (A1, v04)
Live checks skipped (would run): Ad Library, Transparency Center, policy updates, store health-app rules, GBP rate.

## Real outcome & economics
- **Paying subscriber** (trial → paid). Break-even per payer = 12-mo LTV: **₹1,150 India**, £33 (≈₹3,700 at assumed ₹112/£) UK.
- Break-even per trial = LTV × trial-to-paid: India 1,150 × 18% = **₹207**; UK £33 × 31% ≈ £10 ≈ ₹1,150.
- History: ₹14 CPI ÷ 0.4% = ₹3,500/trial ÷ 18% = **₹19,400/payer** (17× break-even).
- **Transaction:** app stores (Play/App Store billing). Platforms see only what Firebase/AppsFlyer send. Reconcile weekly: AppsFlyer purchases vs Play Console/App Store Connect revenue (net of 15% cut, refunds).

## Goal translation & declined asks
- "Maximum installs" → declined. Optimise for **trial_start** (≈50+/week per unit possible); move to purchase/value once ≥50 purchases/week.
- **Declined:** targeting "anxiety, depression, insomnia" and "Are you depressed? Shaant can cure it." Personal-attribute + health policy (both platforms), unsubstantiated cure claim, ethics. **Alternative:** broad Advantage+/App-campaign targeting; benefit copy ("Wind down in 4 minutes", "Sleep routines that stick"); no cure/treat claims. Competitors' "Struggling with anxiety?" hook is policy-risky and saturated: don't copy.
- Honesty: centre forecast ₹2,200/payer > ₹1,150 break-even. 12-month payback unlikely in month 1; goal is to prove cost/trial ≤ ₹300 by day 30.

## Platform split
No trial-optimised history on either platform, so the split is near-equal, each at its comfortable learning level (stated constraint). Google App covers Play/Search/YouTube; Meta suits the Reels-native creative.

## Where the plugin's instructions fell short and I improvised
- No budget rule for Google App campaigns. I used the PMax 1.5× rule; Google's ~10× tCPA guidance is unverified.
- benchmarks.md has no cost-per-trial figure. I derived it from the cost-per-payer row × 18%.
- Neither playbook covers app setup (SKAN/AEM, per-OS App campaigns, Firebase/AppsFlyer linking). iOS handling was improvised.
- The tracking vocabulary is web-only, with no status for "events exist, not linked". I used "Configured, not linked".
- Unclear whether caps apply before or after a planned fix, so I gave both.
- The LTV doesn't say whether it is net of the 15% store cut. I assumed net. The GBP rate is assumed.
- Fixed checkpoint days ignore the 7-day trial lag, so the day-14 read uses partial cohorts.
- Templates assume conversion location, "who answers" and lead CTAs. For apps I mapped these to store, trial lifecycle and "Install now".
- The hard/soft table is about radius, not a second country, so I treated the UK as a scope item.
