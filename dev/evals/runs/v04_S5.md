# S5 — Crumb & Co (Indiranagar bakery) — Paid Ads Plan, Oct 2026

**Real outcome:** paid custom-cake orders (₹1,200-3,000, assume AOV ~₹2,000) confirmed on WhatsApp; secondary: Swiggy/Zomato orders. **Economics (assumed 60% gross margin, client to confirm):** ~₹1,200 contribution/cake → break-even ₹1,200 per order; target ≤₹500. **Where it transacts:** WhatsApp (Meta counts the conversation; order confirmed manually) and Swiggy/Zomato (invisible to Meta/Google). No pixel, no site (Linktree only). Reconciliation: campaign code in WhatsApp greeting + WhatsApp Business label "Ad order" + daily sheet tally (count, ₹ value).

**Goal translation:** "More online orders" → WhatsApp custom-cake orders (the only ad-trackable, highest-value path). "More Instagram followers" → not a paid objective now: followers don't pay bills and ₹300/day can't fund two learning units. Alternative: organic reels + creator collabs; deferred paid follower test (D1). Nothing declined on policy grounds.

**Platform split:** Meta 100% (₹300/day). Intent order would favour Google Search, but there is no landing page or tracking, Google can't fund a second unit (≥15 clicks/day), and map-pack "cake shop near me" intent is caught free by Google Business Profile. Stated constraint, not a contradiction.

## Policy
No special ad category (India, food); no securities declaration. Accurate "eggless"/"sugar-free" claims, no health claims; children in birthday imagery only with consent; rum/plum cakes adult-appropriate. Baseline from plugin; live policy check not run.

## Operating constraints
Daily budget + WhatsApp away message outside shop hours with next-morning reply (lifetime scheduling not worth it at ₹300). Named staff replies <15 min. Lead time (24-48h) and daily cake capacity stated in greeting/copy; pause/lower if order book is full before festivals.

## Risks/blockers
1. Budget below learning minimum. 2. Reply speed/owner unknown. 3. No offer defined. 4. Conversation→order rate unknown; manual tally discipline. 5. 3 km pool + saturated reel style among competitors.
