# D1 FreshCrate: staged paid plan (Oct 2026)
Desk run. Live checks not done: Ad Library, Transparency Center, SERP, policy and Diwali dates.

## Real outcome and economics
- Outcome: a serviceable customer who reorders. Installs are not the outcome.
- Contribution/order = 520x14% - 38 - 12 = Rs22.8. 12-month = 7.5x22.8 = Rs171, minus Rs150 first-order discount = **Rs21 per customer = break-even media CAC**. 3:1 allowable = Rs7.
- History: Rs4.5L / (11,800x21% = 2,478 orders) = Rs182 per first-order customer; ~Rs120 if the 34% non-serviceable installs are removed. Planned Rs150 (110-225, derived).
- **Verdict: LOSS** (LTV:CAC ~0.14:1). The Rs60 target equals 3:1 on Rs171 before the discount, so it ignores the Rs150 discount. Even with no discount, break-even CAC is Rs171.
- Transaction happens in the app. Platforms see installs only (no first order, no pincode). Reconcile weekly against backend orders by campaign code.

## Goal translation
- "20,000 at Rs60": Rs12L / Rs150 is about 8,000 (history: 6,600). Unrealistic. Restated as cost per reordering customer, contribution-positive, staged.
- Declined: "10 minutes, guaranteed" (22% within 10 min, median 17 = misleading). Alternative: "~17 min median, last 30 days" per store. A 10-minute claim only where a store hits >=90% (improvised). Sign-off: CEO + legal.
- Declined: installs as the goal (21% order). Serviceable-install is a diagnostic only. Sign-off: CFO/CEO.
- Adopted: finance's contribution goal; ops' 7-9pm request.

## Operating constraints
Stock-outs 12% at 7-9pm: M1/M2 use lifetime budgets with schedule off 19:00-21:00; Google ad schedule off; resume when stock-outs <5% (improvised). Ads only for in-stock lines and live stores; pause per store at capacity.

## Key risks / blockers
1. Launch blocker: first_order and pincode not sent; fix (~14 days).
2. Loss-making unit economics.
3. Delivery reality vs promise.
4. Diwali CPM +20-40%.
5. 12-month LTV unproven for paid cohorts.
6. Advantage+ app geo limits (hence manual).
7. National apps at Rs100-200 off.
