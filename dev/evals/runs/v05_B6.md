## Outcome and economics
- Real outcome: CRM-verified deposit (assumed = one booking). Booking = ₹16k x 2.6 = ₹41.6k; 22% net = **~₹9,150 break-even per deposit** (if deposit is per person: ₹3,520).
- History: Meta ₹26L/1,940 = **₹1,340**; Google ₹14L/410 = **₹3,415** (17% of CRM deposits). GA4's 61% is brand search harvesting Meta demand; ignore it for allocation.
- Transaction: site/WhatsApp/phone enquiry, counsellor, deposit. Platforms see Lead/Purchase (CAPI) or form submit only; Google needs deposits imported offline (gclid).

## Goal translation
- "80% to Google, Meta is likes" declined (data, not policy): Meta is 2.5x cheaper per CRM deposit; non-brand Google is small (₹3.2L/mo / ₹60 CPC = 5,300 clicks it cannot absorb well). Goal is deposits, not engagement.
- Alternative: Google ~20% (brand defence + non-brand test); settle with a day-30 incrementality test (brand-pause/geo holdout), judged on CRM cost per deposit.

## Platform split
₹4L / 30.4 = ₹13,150/day. Meta ₹9,300 (71%), Google ₹2,600 (20%), reserve ₹1,250 (9%). Best channel gets what it can absorb: Meta is +30% vs its ₹7,130/day history; reserve feeds Meta in +20% steps, unspent carries to Jan-Mar peak.
