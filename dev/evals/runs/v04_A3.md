# A3 — CareBridge Staffing — Paid Ads Plan (Oct 2026)
Overall confidence **58/100** (fix blockers first / run as controlled launch). No live checks run (Ad Library, Transparency Center, policy search, ATS/careers-page tag audit would be live in a real run); all policy items are plugin baseline 2026-10-04.

## Real outcome, economics, transaction
- Real outcome: **placed RN** ($9,500). Proxy we manage to: **qualified applicant** (active TX/compact RN + ≥1 yr acute), recruiter-confirmed in Bullhorn.
- Value per qualified = $9,500 ÷ 6 = **$1,583 = break-even**. Client target $18,000 ÷ 200 = **$90/qualified** (17× headroom). 200 qualified ≈ 33 placements.
- Transaction: external ATS (Bullhorn). Meta sees ATS thank-you page only (pixel not yet installed: *configured, firing unverified*). Google sees nothing on ATS. Reconcile weekly: campaign code/UTM in Bullhorn source field → leads → qualified → submitted → placed per campaign.
- Honest flag: 200 in 60 days is above our centre forecast (~180); placements lag past day 60.

## Goal translation & declined asks
- "Applications" → Meta **Leads (higher-intent instant form with knock-out questions)**, Google **Search leads** (lead form asset + calls). Not ATS completions: too few events to learn on $300/day.
- **Declined:** women 24–40, exclude over-50s, 10-mile radius. Breaks Meta Employment special ad category (US), Google US employment personalised-ads rules, and US age/sex discrimination law. **Alternative:** all ages/genders, metro targeting ≥15 mi (Houston +25 mi, Dallas–Fort Worth +25 mi); filter by job requirements asked of everyone: licence, acute experience, **shift preference (nights Y/N)**. Creatives show nurses of mixed ages and genders.

## Platform split
Meta 67% / Google 33%. Meta has client history ($11 CPL; ~$55 per RN-level lead after 80% junk) and a lower forecast cost per qualified ($80 vs $185). Google is high-intent but crowded by job boards/hospitals ($4–9 CPC) and blind to the ATS; funded at its learning minimum only.

## Policy decisions
- Meta Employment SAC **required** (US): 18–65+, all genders, ≥15 mi, no ZIP/exclusions/lookalikes, Advantage+ audience only. India securities declaration: N/A.
- Google: no age/gender/parental/marital/ZIP targeting. Lead form asset needs privacy-policy link. Pay/bonus claims client-substantiated.

## Operating constraints
Two recruiters, 48 h screen = too slow. Fix: auto SMS/email on submit, first call ≤4 business hours, thank-you screen calendar booking. Meta always-on (daily budget, auto-reply); Google call asset scheduled Mon–Fri 8–6 CT. Capacity: ~8 leads/day is manageable for two recruiters.

## Key risks/blockers
1. Recruiter speed (48 h) and no stated offer (pay range/sign-on bonus).
2. Leads ≠ ATS applications: recruiters must push form leads into Bullhorn; install Meta pixel on ATS thank-you page (audience + secondary event).
3. Google CPC volatility vs $100/day minimum; job-board competition.
4. Non-RN leakage (CNA/students) despite knock-outs.

## Where the plugin was missing/unclear
- Benchmarks/budget rules in INR only; no US recruitment row → derived in USD; "Deferred ₹" expressed in $.
- Unclear whether Meta "people living in" location type and hard boundary survive under the Employment SAC; assumed yes at ≥15 mi.
- No guidance on legitimate job-requirement screening (night shifts) as the alternative to age proxies; improvised.
- Google tracking when the ATS cannot be tagged: plugin only says "optimise to last trackable step"; I chose lead form asset + calls as primary, while the playbook says lead form assets are low quality by default (resolved with knock-out questions).
- Search budget rule with a wide CPC range: no rule for which point to test; used midpoint and flagged upper end.
- Rubric has no level for client-supplied competitor CPCs (scored 4) or for qualified-rate assumptions.
- Merging Houston+Dallas: scope §3 allows it when policy and language match; judged them one market.
- The day-30 checkpoint can't read placements (lag); used submittals as interim.
