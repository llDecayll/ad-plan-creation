# B1 VOLT-X plan (quality gate run; all ★ lines pass)

No live checks done (Ad Library, SERP, policy, GTM); each would run live before launch. Budget ₹12,00,000/day x 42 days = ₹5.04 cr, joint pool.

## Real outcome and economics
- Real outcome: incremental cans sold through (rate of sale, repeat) in the 40 live cities. Platforms cannot see it.
- Contribution per can unknown. Labelled 50% assumption (~₹30 of ₹60 MRP); cost evidence capped at 50. Ask for real margin.
- Break-even on the assumption: ₹5.04 cr ÷ ₹30 ≈ 1.68 crore incremental cans (~4 lakh/day). Six-week payback is unlikely, so repeat/LTV must carry the case.
- Transaction: modern trade, kirana, quick-commerce. Meta/Google see none of it. Reconcile via matched-city sell-out lift, brand lift, brand-search trend.

## Goal translation, declined asks
- "Conversion campaign": declined as the goal. Distribution-led launch, no online conversion, so Awareness (reach and frequency) judged on sell-out.
- 50% of spend on locator-click "conversions": declined; capped at 5% as diagnostic.
- "Gamers 13+", "16-24": declined. Caffeinated energy drink means 18+ only, no minor-centric creative. Alternative: adult gamers/students 18+.

## Platform split
Google/YouTube ₹6,90,000 (57.5%), Meta ₹5,10,000 (42.5%). No history, so intent order: no search demand for a new brand, so demand creation. YouTube bumper CPM (₹120-250) is below Meta reach CPM (₹150-300); competitors dominate YouTube and Reels, so both are needed.

## Deferred, Other channels
- Deferred: video-viewer retargeting ₹60,000/day (from A/B; trigger 50k+ viewers, ~day 14); non-stocked cities ~₹30,000/city/day (trigger stock live); post-launch sustain (₹ from day-30 read). Client decides each.
- Other channels (plan separately): quick-commerce in-app ads (Blinkit/Zepto), 10-15% of budget (₹1.2-1.8L/day), the only attributable sales channel; retail media, creator fees, OTT/CTV, sampling.

## Policy
- Special ad category: none (not housing/employment/credit); audience India, not required, do not opt in; verify live. Securities declaration N/A.
- Energy drink: 18+, no performance/health/consumption claims, no "unlimited". Disclaimer "Not recommended for children, pregnant or lactating women" on every creative. Check FSSAI/ASCI, Google age-restricted rules live.

## Operating constraints
No enquiries, so no reply schedule. Pause by city on stock-out. Hold out 4 matched cities 3 weeks as control (client decision). Diwali (~8 Nov) CPM +20-40%. Budget changes ≤20% per 3-4 days.

## Risks
1. No sell-through data (only sell-in); lift read fails without a sell-out source.
2. CEO's measurability expectation vs awareness.
3. ₹12L/day absorption for a brand with 1.2k followers and 0 YouTube; frequency and CPM will climb.
4. Meta Pixel Unknown; GA4/locator clicks Configured, firing unverified.
5. Creator age and claims compliance. 6. Margin unknown.
