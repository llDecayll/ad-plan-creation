## Where the plugin was missing, unclear or contradictory
- **Benchmarks:** no UK or e-commerce ROAS/CPA data; only India D2C × 6-15× multiplier, giving an unusably wide range (£20-127). I improvised a centre of £30.
- **ROAS/MER translation:** the playbook gives break-even formulas, but there's no protocol for a client ROAS target or for value-bidding thresholds (tROAS minimum conversions, Advantage+ sales existing-customer cap %; I chose 15%).
- **Consent-gated tracking** isn't in the tracking-audit vocabulary or the rubric's Tracking factor (both are lead-centric); I scored 5.
- **Hard-boundary table** covers services only; for e-commerce delivery I chose "people living in".
- **Merchant Center remediation** (GTIN/identifier_exists, misrepresentation review) and brand-campaign bidding (target impression share) aren't covered.
- **Forecast-width rules vs caps:** a score of exactly 60 with the benchmark-only cap gives ±35% under the 60-79 band; it's ambiguous whether a capped score should widen to ±50%.
- **Creative/RSA rule:** the quality gate requires 5 RSA angle sets even for a tiny brand campaign, which isn't useful.
- **Currency and region:** the plugin is ₹- and India-centric (₹ thresholds, WhatsApp defaults), so I converted everything to £.
- **Q4 ramp:** the playbook says "plan ramps" but the brief fixes the monthly budget, so I made the ramp a client decision.
- **Quality-gate scope:** creatives are given for the main campaign only, per the runner word limit; the full plan would need 5 per funded campaign.
