# C3 Mehta & Associates ad plan (from scenario intake; no live checks run)

**Headline: 39/100 now, about 57 after fixes. Do not spend until tracking, lead handling and compliance sign-off are done.** Live checks still owed: Ad Library, SERP, GTM, ICAI/SEBI/Meta/Google policy.

## Outcome, economics, transaction
- Outcome: qualified wealth client (₹50L+ investable, met, onboarded); for tax, a retained GST/ITR client.
- Fee 0.75% × ₹70L = ₹52,500/yr per client. The "₹7 crore" is AUM, not revenue: 10 clients ≈ ₹5.25L/yr vs ₹5.4L spend, so year-1 ROAS ≈ 1x, not 130x.
- Margin unknown: labelled 50% (cost evidence ≤50). Break-even per client ≈ ₹26k year 1, ≈ ₹79k at 3 years (assumed).
- History: ₹1.26L ÷ 11 qualified = ₹11.5k each; ÷ 2 clients = ₹63k each (tiny sample); close 18%. Targets: ≤₹10k per qualified, ≤₹70k per client.
- Transaction is offline after 2-3 meetings over 3-4 months. Platforms see only form/call/WhatsApp taps. Reconcile weekly in a sheet by campaign code, UTM and gclid.

## Goal translation, declined asks
- "10 clients this quarter" is unreachable from new spend (cycle). Plan 3-10 clients over ~7 months, 0-3 closed inside the quarter.
- Partner B: GST/ITR funded as a 12.5% test; ITR peak push deferred to June-July.
- Declined (sign-off: managing partner and IA compliance officer):
  - "12-14% assured": banned. Use fee transparency and a market-risk line.
  - Named testimonial with returns: use credentials and no-returns process stories.
  - "Pune's No.1 CA": use verifiable registrations and years.
  - Competitor comparison: use generic "questions to ask any adviser".
- Blocker: ICAI limits advertising and solicitation. Get written confirmation of permitted ad form first.

## Platform split (₹6,000/day = ₹1.8L ÷ 30)
Meta 50%, Google 50%. Meta has the only history (low quality); Search has none but higher intent; derived cost per qualified overlaps (Meta ₹4.5-12.5k, Search ~₹5-20k). So split evenly and re-weight at day 14 on real outcomes. Wealth 87.5%, tax 12.5% (inside 15-25% cap). No retargeting (no pool).

Forecast 90 leads/mo (60-130), qualified 8-20%, ₹4.5-12.5k per qualified, 1-3 clients/mo. Form: assets band, timeline, city, "just exploring" disqualifier.

**C3 Google Search, intent capture.** GST retainer plus throttled ITR/tax-audit ad group, Pune+Mumbai, Presence, Max clicks until verified. ₹750/day. Sum: 3,000 + 2,250 + 750 = ₹6,000 ✓. Wealth quarter: ₹4.73L ÷ 3-10 clients = ₹47k-1.6L per client (mid ~₹79k, near break-even).
