Reconcile via UTMs/gclid in the call-centre sheet, weekly dispositions, ERP admission export, offline import.

**Goal translation.** "Fill 720 seats" is partly mis-set. Ads forecast about 147 admissions (about 20%). The rest needs KCET/COMEDK allotments, portals and referrals. Management-seat count requested. Phases: Oct–Feb nurture (about 21%), Mar–Jul peak (79%), reserve for results days.

**Real-outcome total:** about 147 admissions (75–220) from ₹41.7L, about ₹28k per admission, against the ₹32k target. The assumed funnel rates are labelled "assumed".

**Deferred.** Gulf NRI parents (soft): separate Meta campaign, about AED 210/day (7× AED 30) ≈ ₹5,000/day ≈ ₹7.5L for 150 days (AED≈₹23). Client decides; UAE permit and callback cover (7:30–16:30 UAE) needed.
**Other channels:** OOH/billboards (Chairman), Shiksha/CollegeDunia/Careers360, Google Business Profile, alumni LinkedIn, WhatsApp broadcast to old enquiries, PUC counselling events (outside plugin).

**Policy.** Meta special category: education is not covered, India audience, so not required and do not opt in. Gulf audience is optional, also do not opt in. India securities declaration: no. Google: no personalised-ads restriction. Baseline 2026-10-04; live check pending. Capitation-fee law: disclose the approved fee structure; no "direct admission" or "guaranteed seat" copy. Minors: target parents only. DPDP consent line on forms.

**Operating constraints.** Call centre is 9–6, Mon–Sat. Search ads run 24/7, forms promise "a call within 2 working hours (9–6, Mon–Sat)", and the call asset is scheduled Mon–Sat 9–6. Meta uses a daily budget with an auto-reply and callback-slot question; Sunday leads are called Monday 9:00. Peak load is about 48 leads/day for 9 agents; cap spend if the queue exceeds 24 hours.

**Risks/blockers.** Chairman claims sign-off; `generate_lead` firing unverified; ERP untrackable (CRM dispositions mandatory); Meta pixel/CAPI unknown; call-centre language cover unknown; ads cannot fill 720 seats alone; Mar–Jul CPC rise.
