# S4 Skyline Residences (Pune) - plugin output, run r1

**Assumptions:** pooled ₹15,000/day; website, offer, page speed not given. Not run live: Ad Library, SERP/keyword volume, policy search, PageSpeed; FX assumed ₹24/AED, ₹85/USD.

## 1. Real outcome and economics
- Outcome: **attended site visit in CRM**, then booking. History: Meta form ₹350 / 3% = ₹11,667 per visit; Google ₹1,100 / 12% = ₹9,167 per visit.
- Targets: Google ≤₹9,200, Meta ≤₹11,700, Pune blended ≤₹10,200; kill ceiling 1.3x.
- Break-even not computable (no visit-to-booking rate or unit margin); client to supply.
- Transaction is offline; platforms see only the lead. Reconcile weekly: CRM visits by campaign code/UTM, plus Google offline import (gclid).

## 2. Goal translation
"Site visits" is the right goal and is honoured; judged by cost per visit, not per lead. No policy-breaking ask. One adjustment: "hard boundary" plus NRIs conflict, so separate per-country campaigns (hard on Pune; "living in" country for NRIs). USA deferred.

## 3. Platform split
Google ₹6,500 (43%), Meta Pune ₹5,500 (37%), Meta UAE test ₹3,000 (20%). Google gets the larger Pune share (54%) as it is cheaper per visit, but is capped at ₹6,500: volume is unverified (implied CPC ₹35-55 means ~120-185 clicks/day) and Meta needs ≥7x CPR to learn. UAE is scope, funded as a capped test.

- #1: 5.9 leads/day, 0.71 visits/day = **~21 visits** (14-29), ₹9,167/visit (history).

- #3: ~93 leads; visit or video-tour booked at assumed 3% = ~3, ₹30-45k each (2-4x Pune cost; rough).

## 6. Policy
- Housing topic. India/UAE audiences: special ad category **not required; do not opt in**. If Ads Manager prompts, accept and confirm plan works under restrictions (no age/gender, no lookalikes).
- USA: **required** (age 18-65+, ≥15-mile radius, no lookalikes); Google also bars age/gender/ZIP. Split and deferred; live check pending.
- RERA number on every ad/form; no appreciation or return promises; India securities/investments declaration answered accurately ("no" unless fractional or return products).
- UAE advertising permit for marketing foreign property: unverified, open item.

## 7. Operating constraints
- Call centre 9am-9pm IST, 5-minute SLA. Meta stays always-on (daily budget) because evenings are peak browsing. Overnight leads are first in queue at 9:00; call asset scheduled to hours.
- UAE: 9am-9pm IST = 7:30am-7:30pm GST. The form offers morning and 5-7:30pm callback slots; later evenings uncovered (ask for a late shift or WhatsApp). USA: 9pm IST = 11:30am ET, so US evenings fully uncovered (a reason for deferral).

## 8. Key risks
Offer/page unknown; 3% past visit rate; CRM-to-Meta link unconfirmed; Google volume ceiling; Diwali CPM +20-40%; UAE permit; slow NRI visits.

## 12. Gaps and improvisations
- Diaspora guidance contradicts: scope-and-channels says "living in", policy-watch v0.5 says "interest or recent location". I used "living in".
- Two UAE benchmarks conflict (AED anchors vs 3-6x India); used their overlap.
- No NRI outcome definition or benchmark; invented the 3% proxy, labelled assumed.
- Pixel "Lead on form" is the website form; instant forms use native leads and CAPI is unmentioned; scored as unverified.
- Conversion leads via CAPI is unreachable at ~14 visits/month; no rule for this, kept Leads.
- No rule for prime-hours scheduling vs ad scheduling; chose always-on.
