# B1 VOLT-X: Paid Ads Plan (run v05)
Live checks the plugin would run (not done): Ad Library/Transparency Center, Meta/YouTube reach estimators, GTM/pixel audit, ASCI/FSSAI caffeine rules, Diwali (~8 Nov) CPMs.

## 1. Real outcome and economics
Outcome: incremental cans sold through (rate of sale per outlet, repeat purchase), not clicks. Transaction: modern trade, kirana, Blinkit/Zepto; platforms see none of it. Brand has only weekly sell-in (not consumer demand). Spend ₹12L x 42 = ₹5.04 Cr = 84 lakh cans at ₹60 MRP (2 lakh incremental cans/day) just to equal MRP revenue; margin not supplied (ask; none invented). Plainly: six-week media will not pay back; judge on lift and repeat.

## 2. Goal translation
"Conversion campaign" declined as primary: distribution-led launch = Awareness (reach and frequency). Measurability via brand lift, matched-city sell-out lift (cities in 3 weight clusters, ±30%; optional 3-city holdout), brand-search/Trends; locator clicks diagnostic only.
Declined: (a) "16-24" and "gamers 13+": age-restricted product, adults 18-34 only (18-24 students allowed); (b) 50% on locator-click conversions: weak proxy, capped at 5% retargeting.

## 3. Platform split
Meta ₹6.6L (55%) / Google ₹5.4L (45%), each including a ₹60k test slice (10% test pool total). No history, no search demand for a new brand, so demand creation leads. Meta slightly higher: creator partnership ads plus engaged-video pools for retargeting. YouTube matches incumbents' bumper frequency. Re-split Day 14 on cost per lifted/incremental outcome.

## 5. Deferred / Other channels
Deferred: rest of India (no stock; ~₹15-30k/day per added city); site-visitor retargeting (pool tiny; trigger: few thousand/30 days); Week 7+ sustain (client decides).
Other channels (outside plugin): Blinkit/Zepto/Instamart in-app ads (only sales-attributed option, 10-15% ≈ ₹1.2-1.8L/day), modern-trade retail media and kirana POS, CTV/OTT cricket, 18+ campus/gym sampling.

## 6. Policy
Special category: none (not housing/employment/credit); India audience; not required, do not opt in; India securities declaration N/A; baseline 2026-10-04, re-check live. Age-restricted: 18+, Audience Network off, creators proven ≥85% adult audience, adult cast, warning legible; 32 mg/100 ml = 80 mg/can; no performance claims.

## 7. Operating constraints
Ads only where stock is live (city pause if out of stock); weekly city-level sell-out feed required; locator kept current; quick-commerce covers only some cities; Diwali CPM spike; always-on.

## 8. Risks/blockers
Minor-skew gaming inventory; no sell-out data; ads ahead of shelf; Meta pixel/GA4-to-Ads link unconfirmed; payback gap; approvals (submit by 20 Oct).
