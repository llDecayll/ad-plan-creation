# B5 Akshara Foundation: plan (RUN v05)
*Not done live (plugin would): Ad Library, SERP, tracking blocker check, policy search. Policy = baseline 2026-10-04.*

**Realism:** Rs1.2 Cr = ~5,000 gifts at Rs2,400. Paid budget to 31 Mar is ~Rs9L. Forecast first-gift return ~1.9x, so paid + Grants plausibly raise Rs15-30L (rough). Rs1.2 Cr needs ~Rs60L+ spend (7x budget, CPA rising).

## 1. Real outcome and economics
- Outcome: completed Razorpay donation (net of failed/duplicate), then repeat gift. First gift Rs2,400; 12-month ~Rs2,930 (22% repeat; assumes repeat = average gift). Break-even cost/donation Rs2,400; target <=Rs1,300. Razorpay fees not supplied (ask).
- Transaction: own site -> Razorpay -> /thank-you (GA4 `purchase`). Trackable once linked; reconcile weekly with Razorpay (>20% gap = broken tracking; dedupe on transaction ID). 80G receipt email = repeat-donor list.

## 2. Goal translation / declined asks
- **Declined: NRI ads (US/UK/UAE/SG).** No FCRA; foreign-national/OCI gifts may be foreign contribution. Needs legal confirmation. Alternative: email/WhatsApp to existing NRI supporters after counsel opinion; ask Razorpay to disable international cards meanwhile.
- **Declined: named, identifiable children + "her family is starving."** Privacy/dignity, unverified claim. Alternative: guardian-consented non-identifying imagery, composite story labelled as such, verified stats.
- **Declined: new Ad Grants account.** Circumvention. Appeal the suspension, fix cause.

## 3. Platform split and why
Meta Rs3,400/day (68%), Google paid Rs1,600/day (32%), Grants Rs0 cash. Intent order says Search first; Search gets less because niche volume is small, Grants takes the broad keywords free, and Meta's learning minimum (7x CPR = Rs3,360) needs the rest. Total Rs5,000 x 30 = Rs1,50,000.

## 4. Campaigns
**C1 Google Ad Grants Search** (separate Grants account; in-kind, Rs0). Stage: capture. Objective: donations. Location: India, Presence (hard). Bid: Max conversions on imported GA4 purchase. Keywords: multi-word intent ("donate for child education India", "80G donation online"), brand terms to lift CTR; no single words; strong negatives. Budget check: Rs0 cash; volume-limited, not cap-limited. Forecast: 600-1,500 clicks/mo x 1.5-4% = 9-60 donations/mo (~30, Rs72k); rough. Real outcome: Rs0 cost per donation, CTR must stay >=5%.
**C2 Google paid Search** (separate paid account; verify live). Stage: capture. Keywords: "donate now/80G/sponsor child education"; negatives shared with C1 to avoid overlap. India, Presence. Max conversions, tCPA at ~30 conv/mo. Rs1,600/day. Check: CPC ~Rs40 (derived), CR 2.5% -> CPA Rs1,600; budget = 1x CPA (pass), 40 clicks/day >=15. High end CPA Rs4,000 fails (downside). Forecast: ~30 donations/mo (Rs72k); CPA Rs625-4,000.
**C3 Meta Sales, website** (Advantage+ audience; new donors). Stage: prospecting. Conversion location: website (Razorpay). Event: custom `begin_donation` (checkout opened), switch to Purchase once >=50/week. Highest volume. India "people living in", expansion off, Advantage+ placements, always-on. **Rs3,400/day.** Check: checkout-open ~Rs480 (derived, Rs350-650) x7 = Rs3,360 <= 3,400 pass; at Rs650 = 5.2x, learning-limited. Forecast: ~7 opens/day x 30-45% complete = 2-3 donations/day, ~80/mo (55-115), cost/donation Rs900-2,200 (derived), ~Rs1.9L raised/mo. Launch blocked until pixel+CAPI verified.
