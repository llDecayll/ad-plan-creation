# B6 TrekNest plan (no live checks run: ad library, SERP, policy, tracking would be checked live; baseline used)

## Outcome, economics, transaction
- Real outcome: CRM-verified deposit (booking). Assumed per group booking: 2.6 x Rs16k x 22% = **Rs9,152 contribution** (break-even cost/deposit).
- History: Meta Rs26L/1,940 = **Rs1,340**; Google Rs14L/410 = **Rs3,415** (2.5x worse). Blended Rs1,702.
- Transaction: enquiry on site/WhatsApp/phone, deposit paid after counsellor chat. Platforms see Lead/Purchase (CAPI) only. Reconcile via campaign codes and CRM.
- GA4 last-click (61% Google) is rejected as evidence; it credits brand search for Meta-assisted demand. CRM source is probably click-based too, so Google's true incremental cost is likely worse.

## Goal translation (client asks declined)
- "80% to Google, Meta is likes": declined (data, not policy). CRM shows Meta gives 83% of deposits at 39% of the cost. Search absorption is limited (non-brand CPC Rs35-90, small volume).
- Alternative: Google gets ~25%, plus a funded Search scale-test (G2 to Rs4,000/day if it earns it, see table) and a brand-search holdout to settle attribution.

## Platform split (daily Rs10,500 = Rs3.2L/month; Rs0.8L/month banked for Jan-Mar peak, client to approve)
Meta Rs7,900 (75%), Google Rs2,600 (25%). Reason: Meta best cost per deposit and can absorb about its last-year level (Rs2.4L vs Rs2.2L/month); Google funded for brand defence plus what Search can absorb.

## Five creative angles (M1)
1. Pain: crowded, over-priced group treks vs small groups with real leaders.
2. Proof: Reel of a real batch summit, with ratings and the count of treks run.
3. Offer: Rs deposit holds a winter-batch seat, seats left.
4. Authority: trek-leader and safety protocol walkthrough.
5. Objection-killer: first-timer, fitness plan, refund/cancellation terms.
