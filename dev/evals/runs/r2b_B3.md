Allowable cost per ticket = price x margin; margin unknown, so labelled 50%: GA NZ$44, VIP NZ$94. Last year's 5.1x reported ROAS, less 30%, is about 3.6x, or roughly NZ$25-28 per ticket. Transaction: Eventfinda (third party); platforms see only pixel/GA4 events. Reconcile weekly from the sales export, using UTMs/codes if allowed.

**Goal reality.** Pace-implied total about 5,000 (2,100 = 42% of final on last year's curve); bottom-up about 3,500 (3,100-4,100). 6,000 is not achievable on NZ$25k.

**Declined asks** (sign-off: client's festival director):

- "Mums with kids" on the VIP ad: declined (parental-status targeting; alcohol ad must not be minor-centric). Alternative: GA day-out creative, "under-18s free with an adult, tastings 18+", no children in alcohol visuals.
