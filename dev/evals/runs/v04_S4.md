# S4 Skyline Residences: Paid Ads Plan (v04)
Not checked live today: policy, SERP, Ad Library. Research comes from the scenario.

**Outcome and economics.** The result that matters is a **site visit**, which leads to a booking (₹85L to ₹1.4Cr). The transaction happens at the site office and is recorded in the CRM. The platforms only see the lead.
- Past cost per site visit: Google ₹1,100 ÷ 12% = **₹9,167**. Meta ₹350 ÷ 3% = **₹11,667**.
- Break-even is an assumption, because margin and close rate were not given. If marketing gets 1-2% of the sale value and 10% of visits book, a visit is affordable at ₹8.5k-28k.
- **Target: ₹12,000 per site visit.**
- Reconciliation: gclid, UTMs and form ID go into hidden CRM fields. Weekly CRM report of leads, qualified leads and visits per campaign.

**Goal translation.** The site-visit goal is the right one. Visits are too few to bid on (about 40 a month), so each platform bids on leads and receives the CRM stages back:
- Google: enhanced conversions, plus offline import of "qualified" and "site visit" as secondary goals with value rules.
- Meta: a higher-intent form now, moving to conversion leads through the CRM and CAPI.
- No client request broke policy. One conflict: a "hard" boundary on Pune would exclude NRIs. Each market is therefore its own hard-boundary campaign.

**Platform split.** Google gets ₹10,500 (70%) and Meta gets ₹4,500 (30%), because Google's cost per visit is lower. Meta is kept at its learning minimum because Hinjewadi search demand may run out at higher spend. If Google's impression share lost to budget is above 20% at day 14, move Meta money to Google.

Meta form: budget band, configuration, own use or investment, timeline (includes "just exploring"), home loan, visit slot. Adds a DPDP purpose line and privacy link. UTMs follow url-parameters.md.

**Deferred**
- NRI-USA: needs ₹10k-22k/day on Google (derived CPA ₹8.8k-22k) or ₹20k-49k/day on Meta. The housing special ad category is required there. The time-zone mismatch means only scheduled callbacks are possible. Client to decide.
- Meta UAE: ₹7k-15k/day.
- PMax Pune: ₹1,650+/day once there are 30+ conversions a month.
- Meta retargeting: once the pool is large enough.
- YouTube/Demand Gen video tours for NRIs.

**Other channels** (outside this plugin): 99acres, MagicBricks and Housing.com with their NRI sections (15-25% of spend); channel partners and Dubai NRI brokers; NRI expos; GBP for the site office; a WhatsApp broadcast to past-project leads.

**Policy**
- Meta housing special ad category: **not required** for India or the UAE, so don't opt in (if forced, accept it and let the form qualify). Required for the US (deferred).
- Investment declaration: answer accurately; no return claims. Google restrictions apply only in the US and Canada.
- MahaRERA number goes on every ad.

**Operating constraints** (call centre 9am-9pm IST, 5-minute SLA): Meta is always-on and the form promises a call within 5 minutes during hours; late leads are called at 9am. The Google call asset runs 9am-9pm. UAE leads (7:30am-7:30pm GST) pick a callback slot or video tour.

**Risks**
- The landing page was not audited, and the CRM-to-gclid/CAPI link is not built.
- Meta's 3% visit rate may persist, and UAE costs are derived.
- Diwali (early November) is a tailwind; a post-Diwali dip is possible.

## Where the plugin was unclear and I improvised
1. The brief doesn't say whether the budget covers both platforms. I assumed it is the total.
2. The learning-limited tier exists only for Meta. I applied it to Google UAE.
3. The 10-20% test cap conflicts with "every market gets a campaign". UAE got 20% and the US was deferred.
4. Hard boundary versus diaspora: §4 flags the conflict but gives no default. I used one hard-boundary campaign per market.
5. Break-even needs margin and close rate, which the intake never asks for. I assumed them.
6. There is no benchmark for higher-intent forms or their visit rate. ₹400-900 and 3-6% are improvised.
7. There is no guidance on optimising toward low-volume visits (value rules or secondary imports).
8. It's unclear whether the 60 cap covers derived multipliers. I applied it.
9. Brand defence can't be decided without a live check. I used an exact-match placeholder.
10. The gate requires ≥5 creatives per funded campaign, but the word limit means only the main campaign's are shown.
11. Scheduling rules sit under local services. I applied them to real estate.
