# B5 Akshara Foundation: plan (no live checks; all "checked live" items marked TO VERIFY)

**Plain truth first:** ₹1.2 Cr needs 5,000 gifts at ₹2,400. ₹1.5L/mo (₹18L/yr) paid cannot deliver that; derived forecast is about ₹36L/yr first-gift (about 30% of target), plus repeats. Ad Grants and owned channels must carry the rest.

## Real outcome + economics
- Outcome: a completed, reconciled donation (Razorpay captured), then a repeat or monthly gift. First gift ₹2,400; 22% repeat, so LTV about ₹2,930 (assumes same gift size; labelled). Net of Razorpay fee about 2% (assumed, ask).
- Target cost per donation (CPD) ≤ ₹1,500 (first-gift ROAS 1.6); break-even ₹2,350 (₹2,930 on LTV).
- Transaction: own site, Razorpay, /thank-you with GA4 `purchase` (trackable). Meta cannot see it (no pixel). 80G receipts auto-emailed. Reconcile weekly against the Razorpay export by UTM/campaign.

## Goal translation and declined asks
- "₹1.2 Cr, mostly NRIs": the target is reframed as paid-acquired donors plus Ad Grants plus owned retention.
- **Declined: NRI/foreign targeting (US/UK/UAE/SG).** No FCRA registration, so foreign-contribution risk. Alternative: India-only now. Deferred until FCRA is granted and counsel confirms Indian-citizen NRIs only.
- **Declined: named, identifiable child with "Rani, 8... starving".** Privacy, dignity, an unsubstantiated and exploitative claim, and personal-attribute phrasing. Alternative: consented, non-identifying imagery, composite or anonymised stories, and verified programme statistics.

## Platform split (₹5,000/day = ₹1.5L/30)
Meta 60% (₹3,000), Google paid Search 40% (₹2,000). Reasoning: donation is created demand, little intent search; derived CPD similar (Meta about ₹1,100, Google about ₹1,333), and Search volume can absorb little. 40% Search is the default 55% minus the allowed 15-point shift. If Ad Grants is reinstated, Search moves to Grants and paid Google shrinks to the best terms.

## Campaigns
**1. Meta Sales: donor acquisition (journey: conversion/lower funnel)**
- Objective: Sales; location: website (Razorpay page); optimisation: InitiateCheckout (donate click), Purchase tracked secondary. Reason: Purchase at CPR about ₹1,100 needs 7× = ₹7,700/day, unfunded; InitiateCheckout is the closest event with 50+/week. Move to Purchase at 50+/week. Bid: highest volume.
- Location: people living in India, expansion off; Advantage+ audience and placements; Audience Network off.
- ₹3,000/day. Budget check: CPR midpoint ₹350 (range ₹250-500, derived), 7× = ₹2,450 ≤ ₹3,000 ✓ (8.6/day, 60/week). Downside ₹500 passes only as a learning-limited test (3× = ₹1,500).
- Forecast: 8.6 checkouts/day; completion 25-40% (assumed); CPD ₹1,100 (₹625-2,000); ₹90k/mo gives about 82 donations (45-144), about ₹2.0L raised (₹1.1-3.5L), first-gift ROAS about 2 (range 1-4). Real outcome: cost per paid donation and repeat rate.

**2. Google Search: non-brand donation intent (conversion)**
- Search, AI Max off, phrase/exact. Themes: donate for child education 80G; sponsor a child's education India; donate to literacy NGO; tax-saving donation 80G; support rural school; volunteer-vs-donate excluded. Negatives: jobs, volunteer, free, internship, scholarship, govt, admission, topper.
- Bidding: import GA4 `purchase` to Google Ads (primary), Maximise conversions; Presence only: India.
- ₹2,000/day. Check: CPC ₹40 (₹25-60) ÷ CVR 3% (2-5%) = CPA ₹1,333; ≥ 1× CPA ✓; 50 clicks/day ≥ 15 ✓. High-end CPA ₹3,000 fails, so a labelled test until 30 conversions.
- Forecast: 45 donations/mo (20-108), ₹1.1L raised (₹0.5-2.6L).

**3. Ad Grants Search (planned, ₹0 paid, trigger: reinstatement).** Appeal with a compliant rebuild: multi-word keywords, ≥5% CTR, GA4 purchase imported, Max conversions. Never open a new account (circumvention). No competitor-style single words ("education", "charity").

Combined: about 127 donations/mo (65-260), about ₹3.0L (₹1.5-6.2L), blended CPD about ₹1,180.
