# B3 Harvest Table Auckland: Paid Ads Plan (NZ$, flight 7 Oct to 21 Nov 2026)
Live checks (tracking, Ad Library, SERP, volumes, policy) not run; baselines used.

**Real outcome and economics.** Net tickets sold (after refunds) and VIP mix. 3,900 more needed (6,000 minus 2,100). Margin unknown, so labelled 50% assumption: break-even about NZ$44 per GA ticket, NZ$94 VIP. Assumed avg ticket NZ$104, 1.4 tickets/order. Last year NZ$18k x 5.1 = about NZ$92k reported, about 900 tickets (about NZ$20 each, platform-reported, overlaps email).
**Where the transaction happens.** Eventfinda (third party). Meta pixel and GA4 can see purchases; Google Ads tag cannot. Reconcile weekly via promo codes and UTMs.

**Goal translation.** "Sell 6,000" kept as Sales/Purchase on a hard date. Honest forecast: bottom-up about 4,800 total (4,000 to 5,600). Paid supplies about 1,300 of the 3,900; the rest is email/organic/partners. Built bottom-up, not on pace.
**Declined asks.**
- "Unlimited wine, drink all you can": irresponsible consumption. Alternative: "VIP includes 6 wine tastings".
- "18-24 party lovers": 18+ fine, but no party/binge segment. Alternative: broad Advantage+ 18+.
- "Mums with kids" on the same VIP ad: declined (alcohol product, one message per creative). Alternative: separate food-led GA creative; "under-18s free" on ticket page only.

**Platform split.** Meta NZ$18,860 (75%), Google NZ$3,795 (15%), reserve NZ$2,345 (9%). Meta has history (about NZ$20/ticket); Google non-brand derived about NZ$44/ticket, tracking unlinked. Google = brand defence plus bounded test.

**Deferred (client decides).**
- Waikato/BoP: about NZ$230/day (about NZ$5k); trigger: Auckland frequency >3/week at day 14.
- Gap to 6,000: about 1,200 tickets = NZ$36-54k extra (low confidence), or accept about 4,800.
- Demand Gen/YouTube: NZ$3k+ after import verified.

**Other channels.** Email/SMS countdown to 14k list (free); Eventfinda featured listing; winery partners, corporate groups; local PR.

**Policy.** Meta special ad category: none (not housing/employment/credit; NZ audience): not required, do not opt in; checked baseline, verify live. Alcohol: 18+, Audience Network off, no volume claims, adult cast; verify NZ ASA code, alcohol promotion law, Google NZ alcohol policy, Privacy Act consent for list upload.

**Operating constraints.** Self-serve checkout; email-only enquiries so no WhatsApp/forms/calls, FAQ plus auto-reply; NZDT; pause on sell-out; end ads when sales close.

**Risks/blockers.** (1) GA4 not linked to Google Ads: link and import purchase before launch; gclid survival to Eventfinda unverified. (2) Meta Purchase on Eventfinda unverified: Day-0 test event. (3) About 1,200-ticket gap. (4) Alcohol ad review delay: submit 5 Oct. (5) Competing festivals; Ad Library unchecked. (6) Email overlap: judge by promo codes. (7) Margin, order value assumed.
