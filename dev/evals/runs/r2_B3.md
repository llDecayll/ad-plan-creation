# B3 Harvest Table Auckland: Paid Ads Plan (NZ$, flight 7 Oct to 21 Nov 2026)
Live checks (tracking, Ad Library, SERP, keyword volume, policy) were not run; plugin baselines used, marked "verify live".

**Real outcome and economics.** Net tickets sold (after refunds) and VIP mix. 3,900 more needed (6,000 minus 2,100). Margin unknown, so labelled 50% assumption: break-even about NZ$44 per GA ticket, NZ$94 VIP. Assumed average ticket NZ$104 (85/15 mix) and 1.4 tickets per order. Last year NZ$18k x 5.1 = about NZ$92k reported, about 900 tickets (about NZ$20 each, platform-reported, overlaps the 35% email share).
**Where the transaction happens.** Eventfinda (third party). Meta pixel and GA4 can see purchases; Google Ads tag cannot. Reconcile weekly to the Eventfinda export using per-channel promo codes and UTMs.

**Goal translation.** "Sell 6,000" kept as Sales/Purchase on a hard date. Honest forecast: bottom-up about 4,800 total (4,000 to 5,600). Paid supplies about 1,300 of the 3,900; the rest must come from email and organic/partners. Plan is built bottom-up, not on pace.
**Declined asks.**
- "Unlimited wine, drink all you can": irresponsible-consumption and misleading claim. Alternative: "VIP NZ$189 includes 6 wine tastings", verifiable facts only. Competitors' "bottomless" copy is not a benchmark.
- "18-24 party lovers": adults, so 18+ is fine, but no party/binge segment or creative. Alternative: broad Advantage+ 18+, adult-only creative.
- "Mums with kids" on the same VIP ad: declined (VIP is an alcohol product; one message per creative; parental/personal-attribute targeting). Alternative: separate food-led GA "day out" creative. "Under-18s free" goes on the ticket page only, not in ads.

**Platform split.** Meta NZ$18,860 (75%), Google NZ$3,795 (15%), reserve NZ$2,345 (9%). Meta has history (about NZ$20/ticket reported); Google non-brand derived about NZ$44/ticket with tracking not yet linked. Google = brand defence plus a bounded test. Split matches this reasoning.

**Deferred (client decides).**
- Waikato/BoP soft ad set: about NZ$230/day (about NZ$5k for 22 days) at higher CPA; trigger: Auckland frequency >3/week at day 14, funded from reserve.
- Close the gap to 6,000: about 1,200 tickets at marginal NZ$30-45 = NZ$36-54k extra (low confidence), or accept about 4,800.
- Demand Gen/YouTube: NZ$3k+ after import verified.

**Other channels (outside plugin).** Email/SMS countdown to the 14k list (free, 35% of sales); Eventfinda featured listing/newsletter; winery/hospitality partners and corporate groups; local media/PR and what's-on listings.

**Policy.** Meta special ad category: none (not housing/employment/credit; NZ audience): not required, do not opt in; checked baseline, verify live. Alcohol: 18+, Audience Network off, no consumption-volume claims, adult cast, "drink responsibly"; check NZ ASA alcohol code and Sale and Supply of Alcohol Act promotion rules, plus Google alcohol policy for NZ. Privacy Act consent for email-list upload.

**Operating constraints.** Self-serve checkout, so no reply-time dependency; email-only enquiries so no WhatsApp/forms/call assets; FAQ plus auto-reply. NZDT scheduling; push Thu-Fri before the event; pause on sell-out (capacity unknown); end ads when sales close 21 Nov.

**Risks/blockers.** (1) Link GA4 to Google Ads and import purchase before launch (status "Events exist, not linked"); gclid survival to Eventfinda unverified. (2) Meta pixel Purchase with value on Eventfinda (domain verification) unverified: Day-0 test event. (3) About 1,200-ticket gap. (4) Alcohol ad review delay: submit 5 Oct. (5) Two competing festivals, Ad Library unchecked. (6) Email/platform overlap: judge by promo codes. (7) Margin, order value, tickets per order assumed.

3. Offer: "VIP NZ$189 includes 6 wine tastings".

5. Objection-killer: "What's in your ticket", 18+, transport, drink responsibly.

**Improvised (plugin gaps).**
- Rules are in rupees; NZ$ conversions, no event-ticketing CPA benchmark; derived CPA from ROAS using assumed AOV and tickets per order.
- Third-party ticket pixel and domain verification not covered; GA4 import for Google conflicts with the playbook line "only if platform allows tags".
- 20% step cap vs back-loaded ramp: chose staged steps.
- Hard/soft location: test rule says "only if better economics", so deferred.
- NZ drinking age, adult 18-24 "party" segment, parental targeting and under-18 messaging not covered.
- Cost-evidence cap vs history score; incrementality of brand Search unforecastable.
- Reserve handling and email-list consent not specified.
