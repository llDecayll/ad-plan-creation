# A4 — Vidyanidhi Institute of Technology — Paid Ads Plan (Oct 2026 → Jul 2027)

**Real outcome:** management-quota admission with first-year fee paid (₹3.2L/yr, ~₹12.8L over 4 yrs). Proxy chain: enquiry → counsellor-qualified → application (₹1,200) → admission. Target allowable: ≤₹40k per admission (client to confirm) → ≤₹12k per application at 30% app→admission.
**Where it happens:** website form (GA4 `generate_lead` → Google Ads, firing unverified) → call centre → third-party ERP (no pixel) → fee. Reconcile: UTMs + gclid in hidden fields, ERP export matched by phone weekly, offline import of "application" and "fee paid" to Google; Meta leads matched from Leads Center.
**Honesty:** 720 seats from paid ads alone is unrealistic at ₹60L; forecast ~100–500 ad-attributed admissions. Other admissions must come from counsellors, referrals, CET/COMEDK allotment.

## Goal translation & declined asks
- Admissions Director → Leads/Search optimised to enquiries, upgraded to applications via offline import.
- Chairman "#1 in Karnataka, billboard reach" → separate awareness campaigns (C4), KA-weighted, measured by branded-search lift, not leads.
- **Declined:** "100% placement" and "#1/Ranked #1" (NIRF 151–200; placement 64%). Alternative: "64% of eligible 2025 students placed, median ₹X LPA" (client to confirm, with source link), accreditations, labs, industry tie-ups.

## Platform split
Search captures demand that exists (₹45–120 CPCs prove it) → Google 50%; Meta reaches parents who aren't searching, via instant forms (no Meta Pixel) → 37%; 13% reserve released on day-30 rules. Intent order, no history.

## Policy
- Special ad category: **none** (education; India and Gulf not required; not housing/employment/credit). India securities declaration: No. Baseline — would live-check Meta/Google Oct 2026 updates.
- Minors: no targeting under 18; parent-addressed creative; no student-interest targeting.
- Claims as above; avoid "direct admission without exam"/capitation language (state law risk — confirm live).
