# Gulf Motors Select: Ad Plan (AED, pilot-first)
No live checks done (pixel/GTM, Ad Library incl. Arabic, SERP, policy, UAE finance-ad rules): all "verify live". Numbers are derived from scenario facts and plugin anchors.

## Economics
- Real outcome: car sold (visit = leading proxy). Contribution/car = 3,200 + 252 finance (28% x 900) - 400 commission = **AED 3,052** (assumes 3,200 is pre-commission; ask).
- Enquiry-to-sale 20% x 18% = 3.6%. Break-even CAC 3,052 (110/enquiry, 549/visit). **3:1 allowable CAC 1,017** (37/enquiry, 183/visit).
- History (11 sales, source on 40%): portals AED 2,000/sale; Meta forms 2,800/sale (38% unreachable).
- Transaction is in the showroom. Platforms see only the form (~45%); calls/WhatsApp (55%), visits, sales are invisible. Reconcile with per-showroom call tracking, WhatsApp number with campaign codes, mandatory source field, reception visit log, weekly tally, offline import.

## Goal translation
"400 enquiries at AED 100" equals break-even (110) and enquiries are the cheap part (Meta: 420 leads, 5 sales). Reset to cost per visit <= 275 and per sale <= 1,526 (2:1); 3:1 long-run.
**Verdict: planned ~AED 2,200/sale = 1.4:1, break-even/thin. Staged start: 45% pilot (AED 17,936), AED 22,000 parked with triggers.**
Declined (sign-off: GM/CEO above sales head; finance-partner compliance):
- "Accident-free, certified" on all cars: 9/40 had accident repairs. Alternative: per-car claim only where the report is clean; "report shown for every car" (client to confirm); test vs badge.
- "From AED 899/month" without 20% down/balloon: misleading. Alternative: full example (price, down, term, balloon, bank) or lead with car price.
- Advertising sold cars (38% of feed): fix feed first.

Modest imagery; Arabic and English as separate ads; Arabic test (<=10%) once template and handler exist.

## Operating constraints
Hours not given: assumed 9-21, Sat-Sun weekend, reply <=15 min. Google ad schedule to phone hours; Meta daily budget, away message, morning follow-up, scheduled callbacks. Route to nearest showroom (ID to CRM); pause sold cars daily; weight by showroom capacity.

## Blockers
1. Feed 38% sold: daily sync >=95% live or hand-picked cars; else ~1.6x cost per real enquiry.
2. Call tracking, codes, mandatory source field before spend.
3. Claims sign-off.
4. Pixel/CAPI and GA4-to-Ads link unknown.
5. Margin thin: 20% worse costs = break-even.
