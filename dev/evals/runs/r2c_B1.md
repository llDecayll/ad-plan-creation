# B1 VOLT-X launch plan (no live checks; policy/Ad Library/CPM checks flagged "do live")

**Real outcome + economics.** Incremental cans sold through (rate of sale, repeat), not clicks. ₹60 MRP; margin not given, so labelled assumption: contribution ₹30/can (50%, cost evidence capped 50). Pool ₹12L x 42 = ₹5.04cr. Break-even = 1.68cr incremental cans, which is implausible inside 6 weeks. This is a launch investment judged on trial + repeat, and sell-in/sell-out forecasts are needed from the client (ask).
**Transaction:** kirana, modern trade, quick-commerce. Platforms see none of it. Reconcile via matched-city sell-out vs control cities, brand lift, branded-search trend, weekly distributor sell-in by city.

**Goal translation.** CEO "conversion campaign": declined as stated. Distribution-led launch, so awareness (reach and frequency) is right; the "conversion" read is matched-city lift. Sign-off: CEO/CMO.
Declined asks (sign-off CMO/CEO):
- "Students 16-24", "gamers 13+": minors; 18+ only. Alternative: students 18-24, adult gamers.
- 50% on locator-click optimisation: weak proxy. Capped at 5% as a diagnostic. Alternative: lift test.

**Platform split.** YouTube/Google 46%, Meta 44%, reserve 10%. No history and no search demand, so intent order gives upper funnel. YouTube bumper CPM is cheaper (₹120-250 vs ₹150-300) and incumbents own bumper plus Reels, so YouTube leads. Meta takes creator partnership ads. Brand Search 1% (0-2% rule).

**Campaigns** (pool phased: reserve 10% = ₹50.4L at day 14; deployable ₹4.536cr; burst d1-14 = 55% = ₹2.49cr = ₹17.8L/day; sustain d15-42 = ₹2.04cr = ₹7.29L/day; client to approve vs flat ₹12L).
1. **G1 YouTube reach** (awareness): bumper 6s + skippable + Shorts, Target CPM ₹185, no conversion location. Presence, 40 cities, ages 18-44 only, unknown age excluded. Budget ₹2.27cr (burst ₹8.9L/day, sustain ₹3.65L/day). Check: ₹8.9L ÷ ₹185 x 1,000 = 48L impressions/day. Forecast: 12.3cr impressions (9-19cr), unique reach about 1.2-1.5cr.
2. **M1 Meta Reach** (awareness): Reels/Stories/Feed, creator partnership ads, 2-3 frequency cap/week, highest volume. Living in or recently in, 40 cities in 4 stock-zone ad sets, expansion off, min age 18, Audience Network off. ₹1.51cr (burst ₹5.9L, sustain ₹2.4L/day). Forecast: ₹1.51cr ÷ ₹225 = 6.7cr impressions (3.4-10cr), reach about 75L.
3. **M2 Meta ThruPlay** (consideration): creator video, same location, ₹50.4L (burst ₹2L, sustain ₹0.8L/day). At ₹0.5-2 CPV centre ₹1: about 50L ThruPlays (25-100L).
4. **M3 Meta Traffic to locator** (diagnostic): optimise landing page views, ₹20.2L (4%), ₹0.3-0.8L/day. Forecast about 2L LPV at a derived ₹10 (range wide). Diagnostic only.
5. **G2 brand Search**: ₹5L (1%), exact/phrase, report separately.
Real-outcome forecast (hypothesis, label low): deduped reach about 1.8cr x 5-10% trial + repeat = 8-15L incremental cans, i.e. ₹34-63 ad cost per can vs ₹30 assumed contribution. Pays back only through repeat. Festive CPM (+20-40% in Diwali burst, 8 Nov) pushes results to the low half.
**Reach saturation** (assumed addressable adults 18-44 online: YouTube 12cr, Meta 8cr; verify): 1.5% line = ₹4.2L/day (YT), ₹3.4L/day (Meta). Sustain passes (YT 1.3%, Meta 1.4%). Burst fails (YT about 3.2%, Meta about 3.1%), so CPM rises. If CPM runs >30% over plan by day 5, move up to 10% overflow to Other channels.

**Deferred.** (a) Retargeting of video viewers: trigger ≥3,000 viewers, about ₹10k/day, client OKs. (b) Rest of India: no stock, enter city by city once distribution goes live; about ₹1L/day per metro. (c) Quick-commerce in-app ads: ₹50-75L, outside plan budget unless the client allows.
**Other channels:** Blinkit/Zepto in-app ads (only attributable channel; 10-15% recommended), OTT/CTV, in-store, creator seeding (plan separately).

**Policy.** Special ad category: none, not required in India, do not opt in; Google personalised-ads restriction n/a. Energy drink: 18+, adult cast, Audience Network off, pack disclaimer on every creative, no health/performance claims, verify creator audience age, FSSAI/ASCI check live, submit ads by 20 Oct for slower review.

**Operating constraints.** No enquiries, so no schedule or replies. Stock-out: pause by city zone using weekly distributor stock data. Always-on.

**Risks/blockers.** (1) No sell-out data and no lift design: set up matched-city test before 1 Nov. (2) Spend far exceeds the small brand's absorbable reach and likely the break-even. (3) Claim/minor policy. (4) Festive CPM. (5) Stakeholder pressure toward clicks.

- "~50-60% of reach budget" unclear base: I used 55% of deployable (excluding reserve).
- 1.5% saturation rule gives no addressable-population source and no action when budget exceeds it: I assumed populations and allowed the burst to exceed.
- No sell-out, incremental-can or locator-LPV benchmark: hypothesis and derived figures, labelled.
- "Labelled 50%" margin ambiguous (of MRP vs net realisation): used 50% of MRP.
- Brand Search 0-2% and the 5% locator cap overlap: counted brand within the 5%.
- Hard boundary for retail: chose "living in or recently in".
- Awareness tracking when only weekly sell-in exists: scored 5 and did not apply the cap 60.
- No rule for minimum creator audience age or Google age handling.
