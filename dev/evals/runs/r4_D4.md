# D4 Paws & Co: plan (A$9,000/mo = A$296/day, joint pool assumed)
Not run live (would have been): Ad Library, Transparency, SERP, GTM/booking-widget inspection, policy check. All costs are "derived" benchmarks (cost evidence 3). Quality gate run; every star line passes except where noted.

## Real outcome + economics
- Clinics: attended new patient. First-year contribution A$234 (520 x 45%) = break-even CAC. 3:1 allowable CAC A$78. History A$64.5/patient (2,000/31) = 3.6:1, profitable (how "31" was counted is unverified).
- Store: contribution/order A$15.76 (68 x 32% = 21.76, less A$6 shipping). Break-even ROAS 4.3x. WA/NT/remote: 21.76 - 18 = A$3.76, so excluded from paid.
- Transaction: clinics on a third-party booking widget (invisible to platforms; reconcile by call tracking + reception tally by code). Store on own site (purchase double-fires).

## Goal translation / declined asks
- "A$100k at 8x" declined. 8x needs A$12.5k spend (> whole budget). Expected ROAS ~2.7x would need ~A$37k. Alternative: judge by MER vs 4.3x and cost per attended patient. Sign-off: e-store manager + owner.
- Meta 6.1x not trusted: if on the duplicated event, true ~4.5x (6.1/1.35), before platform over-credit.
- "Vet-recommended, cures arthritis": declined (cure claim unsubstantiated; consumer-law/APVMA risk, verify live). Alternative "formulated to support dog joint comfort" once evidence/registration and named vets exist. Sign-off: lead vet + owner. Unfunded.
- 30% off food at Black Friday: margin falls to ~2% (A$1.36/order, -A$4.64 after shipping). No paid push at 30%; use bundles/non-food or a free-shipping threshold. Sign-off: e-store manager + finance.
- Vet's remarketing: approved after the PII fix (Deferred).

## Platform split and why
Clinics A$185 (62.5%) + shared brand A$10 (3.4%), store A$75 (25%, secondary-goal cap), reserve A$36 (12%). Ranked by cost per real outcome: clinic Search proven A$64.5; clinic Meta ~A$80 derived; store first-order CPA ~A$25 > A$15.76 break-even, so staged test. Google 125 (42%), Meta 135 (46%).

Living in or recently in, expansion off, one ad set, 3 radii. **A$60/day**. Check: 3 x CPL 20 = 60 = learning-limited test, 14-day read. Forecast A$1,824: 91 leads (52-228), ~23 attended patients (11-34) at ~A$80 (A$55-130); borderline to 3:1.

Soft Australia, WA/NT/remote excluded. **A$75/day**. Check: 3 x CPA 25 = 75, learning-limited test (high-end CPA 35 fails; pass only at midpoint). Reserve A$36/day (<=15%): release day 14 to clinic Search if impression share lost to budget > 15% and CPA <= A$78; else park.

## Policy / special category
Pet care/vet/retail: not Housing, Employment or Credit; Australia not required; do not opt in (verify live). Competitor names not in copy; "vet-recommended" and "#1" need evidence. Pet-health copy fine (not human personal attributes). Verify veterinary-product rules on Google live.

## Operating constraints
Ad schedule and call assets to clinic hours; callbacks within 15 min; weight clinics by empty slots (ask capacity); pause clinic ads where slots full; Black Friday shipping cut-offs; WA/NT surcharge.

## Key risks / blockers
1. GA4/Meta purchase double-fire (~35%): add transaction_id dedupe; test purchase day 0.
2. Booking confirmation URL carries name and email: PII in URL (guardrail 4 / platform policy). Vendor must supply a clean URL/event; purge GA4 history; tag only afterwards.
3. Unverified count of "31 patients"; unknown store spend and repeat rate.
4. Competitors (Petbarn, Chemist Warehouse, Pet Circle) own food terms: avoid head-to-head food bidding.
