# B2 FreshNest (AED; no live checks)

**Assumptions:** AED 45,000/month = 1,480/day (÷30.4), one joint pool. Margin unknown, so a labelled 50% (cost evidence capped 50). Abu Dhabi = city only. October = AC off-season.

## Outcome and economics
Real outcome: attended paid job plus AMC attach. Contribution per job: AC ≈ AED 210; deep clean ≈ 500 (mid 1,000). AMC adds 1,800 × 50% × attach (unknown) × 40% renewal. Transaction: WhatsApp or phone, then manual Sheets booking. Platforms see only form submits and Meta's native conversations; reconcile weekly by WhatsApp campaign code.

## Goal translation
- "More bookings" = attended jobs. AC at AED 149 is the weakest economics and October is off-season, so lead with deep clean, end-of-tenancy and AMC pre-sale.
- **Declined: Riyadh next month.** No Saudi registration or team (legal risk). Alternative: defer. Sign-off: owner and counsel.
- **Declined: swimwear imagery.** Gulf modesty norms and rejection risk. Alternative: modest "guest-ready home" visuals. Sign-off: owner.

## Split: Meta 60% (AED 890/day), Google 40% (590)
Derived cost per job: Meta 25 ÷ 11.5% ≈ 217. Search: CPC 10 ÷ 8% = CPL 125 ÷ 25% ≈ 500, which is 2.3× worse. Default Search is 55%+, shifted the allowed 15 points to Meta. Search stays for deep clean, AMC and brand defence.
