## Real outcome, economics, transaction
- **Real outcome:** loan disbursed (in app, by partner NBFC). Value ≈ ₹1,800 net per disbursal → **break-even ₹1,800; target ≤ ₹1,200** (33% margin). Confirm ₹1,800 is net of default-guarantee exposure.
- **Funnel (assumed):** CPI ₹38 (client) → install→KYC 20% (12-30%) → cost per KYC ≈ ₹190 → KYC→disbursal 22% approval × ~85% take-up ≈ 19% → **≈ ₹1,000 per disbursal** (rough, ±50%).
- **Where:** Android app; AppsFlyer sees `kyc_complete`, `loan_disbursed` (1-3 day lag, inside Meta's 7-day window). Reconcile AppsFlyer disbursals with the NBFC ledger weekly; >20% gap = treat tracking as broken.

## Goal translation; asks declined
"Disbursals" is the right goal: optimise on `loan_disbursed`, not installs.

| Ask | Problem | Compliant alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "0% interest" | False (APR 18-36% + 2-4% fee) | "APR 18-36% p.a. See your EMI before you accept" |
| "No CIBIL check" | Almost certainly false; misleading | "Check eligibility in minutes" (+"won't affect your score" only if soft pull, client to confirm) |
| "Guaranteed approval" | 22% approval | "Decision in minutes" (client to confirm timing) |
| Target low credit scores | Vulnerability targeting; also cuts approval below 22% | Broad Advantage+; optimise on disbursal so Meta finds approvable users |
Every ad names the NBFC lender (RBI digital-lending disclosure; live check).
