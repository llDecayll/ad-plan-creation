# VOLT-X national launch plan (B1)

## 1. Real outcome, economics, transaction
- Outcome: incremental cans sold through (rate of sale, repeat) in 40 live cities. Not clicks.
- Economics: pool ₹12L/day × 42 days = ₹5.04cr. Margin not given, so labelled 50% of ₹60 MRP = ₹30/can (assumption; brand net revenue is lower). Break-even ≈16.8 lakh incremental cans (~40k/day).
- Transaction: kirana, modern trade and Blinkit/Zepto. Meta/Google see none of it. Reconcile with quick-commerce city dashboards and a retail audit. Sell-in is not sell-out.

## 2. Goal translation, declined asks
- "Conversion campaign": declined as the main goal. Distribution-led launch, so reach and frequency plus matched-city sell-out lift is right. Locator clicks are a diagnostic, capped at 5%. Sign-off: CEO. Alternative: staggered start in 4-6 cities as the control.
- "50% on locator-click conversions": declined with the same alternative. Sign-off: CEO and CMO.
- "Students 16-24, gamers 13+": declined (minors, age-restricted product). Use 18+ only (18-24 students, adult gamers). Sign-off: CMO.

## 3. Platform split
Pool: locator 5% ₹25L, reserve 10% ₹50L, reach ₹429L. YouTube 52% (₹223L), Meta 48% (₹206L).
Why: no history, awareness goal. YouTube CPM is lower (₹120-250 vs ₹150-300) and placement exclusions control age; Meta adds Reels and creator reach.
Phasing: burst days 1-14 = 50% of reach (₹215L, ₹15.3L/day); sustain days 15-42 = ₹214L (₹7.6L/day).
Reserve of ₹50L released at day 14 to the best channel.

**Plan-wide real-outcome hypothesis:** ~164M impressions (121-235M), 25-35M adults reached (assumes 100M addressable). Incremental 4-32 lakh cans (central ~12 lakh), ₹16-126 per can (central ~₹42). Central case is about 30% short of 6-week break-even, so payback needs repeat purchases. Rough.

**Absorption check (FAIL against guide):** daily reach ~4.5% (burst) and 2.2% (sustain) vs the 1.5% guide. Control is weekly frequency ≤4. Park overflow, don't reroute, if frequency >4 or Meta CPM >₹390.

## 5. Deferred and other channels
- Rest of India: no stock. Trigger: distribution live. About ₹30k/day per added city.
- Brand Search: 0-2%, from the reserve if brand queries appear.
- **Other channels:** Blinkit/Zepto in-app ads, the only attributable channel (recommend 10-15%, ₹50-75L, outside plan budget unless the client allows). Creator fees, in-store activation, OTT cricket.

## 6. Policy decisions
- Special ad category: not required (India), do not opt in. India investments declaration: n/a. Google housing/credit/employment: n/a.
- 18+ only. Audience Network off. Google: exclude Unknown age, Made-for-Kids and kids/teen gaming placements. Verify creator audience is ≥90% 18+.
- Pack disclaimer on every creative. No performance or health claims.
- Verify live: FSSAI/ASCI caffeinated-beverage code, Meta/Google 60-day policy changes. 

## 7. Operating constraints
Go live per city only when stock is confirmed; pause by city on stock-out (weekly stock status from distributors). Group cities into tier ad sets.

## 8. Risks and blockers
1. Weekly frequency and absorption (assumed 100M base).
2. No sell-out data, so ask for quick-commerce dashboards.
3. Margin unknown.
4. Creator audience age.
5. Pixel/CAPI unknown.
6. Payback likely short.

## 11. Five creative angles for Campaign C
1. Situation: "same old can" taste fatigue, no efficacy claim.
2. Proof: adult creators' blind taste test.
3. Offer: ₹60 launch deal on Blinkit/Zepto.
4. Authority: label transparency (32 mg/100 ml) plus the disclaimer.
5. Objection-killer: "Find it at your kirana", store-finder end card.

## 12. Plugin gaps and improvisation
- Reserve rule conflicts (10-15% vs ≤10%); I used 10%.
- ₹12L/day vs 15.3L burst: assumed it is a pool average.
- Addressable base: no source for 100M; the 1.5% guide fails at any plausible base and the plugin gives no remedy.
- No awareness formula for the real outcome, so I built the hypothesis myself.
- Locator LPV cost (₹8-20) is derived.
- No Reach & Frequency guidance in the Meta playbook.
- A holdout conflicts with "40 cities hard", so I used staggered start.
- Gate: all ★ lines pass.
