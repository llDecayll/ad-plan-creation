# C6 BloomWell Fertility plan (not live-checked; policy baseline 2026-10-04)

## Outcome and economics
- Real outcome: started IVF cycle via attended consult. Contribution per patient = ₹1.6L x 1.3 x 35% = ₹72,800 (assumed pre-ad-spend).
- Lead to patient 22% x 14% = 3.08% = ₹2,242 per lead. Break-even: ~₹11,200 per attended consult (incl. ₹1,000 fee), ~₹2,300 per lead, ₹72,800 per cycle.
- Transaction: phone (55%, invisible to platforms), booking form (visible), consult and cycle (CRM only). Needs call tracking and weekly CRM reconciliation.

## Goal translation and declined asks
Optimise consult bookings; judge on attended consults and cycles (cycles read day 60-90).
- **"68%" on every ad: declined.** It is clinical pregnancy, under-30s, transfer-only; live birth per started cycle is 31%. Alternative: no rate in ads; landing page shows both with definitions and year; test a "we publish the real number" ad. Sign-off: medical director + legal.
- **Gender-choice Gulf ad: declined.** Sex selection is illegal in India (PCPNDT; verify with counsel); "Struggling to conceive?" breaks personal-attribute policy. Alternative: "Fertility specialists, transparent IVF packages". Sign-off: CEO + counsel.
- **Portal remarketing: declined.** Diagnosis and appointment type are in portal URLs sent to Meta and GA4. Alternative: remove tags from the portal, strip URLs, audit what was sent, build audiences from public pages only. Sign-off: CEO + IT/data owner.

## Platform split (₹30,000/day x 30 = ₹9L)
Search 50% ₹15,000, Meta 40% ₹12,000, reserve 10% ₹3,000. Search form-only: ₹250 CPC / 8% = ₹3,125 per lead = ₹14,200 per attended consult, above break-even. Meta is ~₹5,300 (derived), so I shifted 5 points toward Meta. Search stays 50%: Meta history was poor quality; Search with calls (assumed) is ~₹6,400.

**Forecasts (30 days, ±50%):**
- G1-G3: 4.6 form leads/day at ₹2,250-4,000 CPL. With calls (assumed 55% phone) ~300 leads at ~₹1,400; ~67 attended consults (52-93) at ~₹6,400.
- M1: 225-525 leads at ₹600-1,400 (CPC ₹20-25 / cold CVR 2.5-3%); attended 12-22% = 27-115, centre 60, at ~₹5,300.
- Plan-wide real outcome: ~80-210 attended consults (centre ~127); ~11-29 cycles (centre ~18), arriving over 1-3 months.

## Deferred and other channels
- **Gulf medical tourists (requested):** ₹8,000-16,000/day test (3x derived CPR ₹2,700-5,400), ₹19,000-38,000 standard. Needs legal clearance, Arabic/English desk, time zones. Client decides.
- **YouTube/Demand Gen:** ₹5,000/day; trigger: day-30 targets met.
- **Per-clinic ad sets:** ~₹44,000/day; not fundable.
- **Reserve:** ₹3,000/day, released day 14 to the winner if cost per attended consult ≤ ₹8,000.
- **Other channels:** GBP for 7 clinics (first); Practo/Justdial; gynaecologist referrals; WhatsApp/email nurture.

## Policy and operating constraints
- Special ad category: none (not housing, employment or credit); India audience, not required, do not opt in; verify live.
- Health: no condition-implying copy, no guarantees, no Google remarketing on sensitive health; Meta health-event limits may block M2 (fallback: CRM nurture).
- Ops: 4-hour reply is the biggest leak, target ≤15 min, scripted first call, callback booking. Call assets scheduled to the 6-agent centre's hours (to confirm). ~22 leads/day fits capacity. Local-language creative; campaign codes and hidden gclid/fbclid fields.

## Key risks
Portal health data in tags (blocker); no call tracking/CRM import; rivals bidding near break-even; slow response.

## Five creative angles (M1)
1. Pain: "Planning a family? Get your options in one consult."
2. Proof: "We publish the real number" (31% live birth per started cycle, definition and year; client to confirm).
3. Offer: "₹1,000 first consult, callback within 15 minutes."
4. Process: "Your IVF journey in 5 steps, with your specialist."
5. Objection-killer: "IVF cost in [city]: transparent package, no hidden add-ons."

## Gaps and improvisations
- No IVF playbook or ART Act/PCPNDT/success-rate rules; improvised from Local services and Health.
- Nothing covers health data in pixel/GA4 URLs; treated as an open policy blocker (cap 55) by analogy.
- "Group catchments into city ad sets" conflicts with the Meta 7x CPR minimum; used one multi-pin ad set.
- Cost evidence: CPC plus client CVR exists; scored 4 (rule says 3 for CPC-only).
- "-5 tracking for health" is ambiguous; folded into 4/10.
- Funded retargeting (v0.5.5) conflicts with health remarketing limits; made M2 conditional.
- No guidance for call-heavy Meta; 55% phone share on paid leads is assumed; no "booked rate" benchmark.
- Intake lacks clinics per city and call-centre hours.
