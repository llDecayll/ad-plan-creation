## 1. Real outcome and economics
- **Real outcome:** an installed system sold to an owner-occupier of a house. Gross margin per sale is A$9,500 × 28% = **A$2,660**.
- **Break-even per qualified lead:** A$2,660 × 15% close = **A$399**. Per genuine enquiry, with 75% qualified: **A$299**. Target cost per qualified lead: **≤A$120** (30% of break-even).
- **Actual last month:** A$6,000 ÷ 92 HubSpot enquiries = **A$65**. With ~69 qualified, that is **A$87 per qualified lead**, about 10 sales, about **A$580 per sale**. Google is profitable, but the "$22 lead" is a fiction: 412 reported conversions vs 92 real ones is 4.5× inflation. Also, A$6,000 ÷ 412 = A$14.6, not $22, so the spend figure needs confirming.
- **Where the transaction happens:** form or phone, then a site visit, then a signed quote, all offline. Platforms see the form submit at best, and calls not at all. HubSpot is where we reconcile: gclid/UTM hidden fields, then an offline import of the stages "qualified (owner-occupier house)" and "won".

## 2. Goal translation and client asks declined
- **"Triple the budget" is declined for now.** Tracking is broken: page-views are counted as leads, there is a duplicate primary conversion action, and calls are not tracked. Maximize Conversions has spent 14 months bidding toward people who view the form page. Under the plugin's rule "Never scale on broken data", spend stays flat at A$200/day until the fix is verified. After that, scale in +20% steps toward A$600/day.
- **We will not copy competitors' "Free solar — government pays".** It is misleading under ACCC/Australian Consumer Law, because the STC is a discount, not a free product. "$0 upfront" may only be used if SunGrid has a licensed finance partner and the credit terms are disclosed.

## 3. Platform split and why
- **Month 1: Google 100%.** Search demand is proven, it is the only channel with the client's own data, and cost per qualified lead (A$87) is 4.6× below break-even.
- **Meta is planned for month 2**, when the budget will reach about A$600/day. Reasons it waits: there is no pixel and no Instagram account, the Page has only 2k followers, and the costs would be derived benchmarks only.
- **Split rule:** Google absorbs budget until impression share lost to budget is below 10%, or until cost per qualified lead rises above A$120. Meta gets the remainder.

## 6. Special ad category and policy
- **Meta:** solar install is a grey area under Housing ("home repairs"). Finance offers would fall under Credit. The audience is Australia, so the category is **not required**, and we do not opt in. If Ads Manager prompts for it, accept. The plan still works because the instant form does the qualifying.
- **Google:** no personalised-ads restriction in AU.
- **Claims:** CEC accreditation and warranties need client confirmation. Do not imply any link to Solar Victoria.
- **Privacy:** Australian Privacy Act, so link the privacy policy on the form.
- **India securities declaration:** not applicable.
- [LIVE-CHECK] Meta/Google policy updates for Oct 2026, and current Solar Victoria and federal battery-rebate status.

## 7. Operating constraints applied
- **Capacity:** one rep plus the owner, booking visits within a week, is the scale ceiling. At about 25 visits/week (to be confirmed), **freeze budget if the visit backlog exceeds 7 days**.
- **Calls:** call assets run in business hours only (AEDT). Forms run 24/7, with an auto-SMS acknowledgement and a next-morning callback.
- **Response time:** reply in ≤30 minutes during business hours.

## 8. Key risks and blockers
1. **Tracking fix (blocker), in this order:**
   - GA4 `generate_lead` fires on the thank-you page or form-success event.
   - The Google Ads tag is the single primary conversion; the imported GA4 conversion becomes secondary.
   - Enhanced conversions are on.
   - Call conversions run through forwarding numbers.
   - HubSpot offline import is set up.
2. **Re-learning dip** after switching the primary conversion.
3. **Capacity ceiling.**
4. **Spend/CPL figures inconsistent.**
5. **Depot location not confirmed:** we need it to confirm Geelong is within 80 km.

## 12. Where the plugin was missing, unclear or contradictory (where I improvised)
- **Currency:** benchmarks are in INR, and the gate thresholds say "₹". I converted using India × the AU multiplier (6-15×) ÷ about ₹57/A$, which gives a huge A$21-210 range. I preferred the client's own data.
- **Test cap vs minimum:** the 10-20% test cap conflicts with the Meta minimum of 3×CPR. A$180 is 30% of A$600. I justified it as Google's absorption limit.
- **No rule for doubled primary conversions** or for scoring tracking "pre- vs post-fix". I reported both scores.
- **Solar vs Housing:** policy-watch doesn't say whether solar counts as "home repairs".
- **No capacity-sizing method** for scaling against sales headcount.
- **No guidance for running Meta without Instagram or a pixel.**
- **Overall score with one funded campaign:** the "spend-weighted average" is trivial. It is unclear whether planned month-2 spend should count.
- **The "$22 CPL" input is inconsistent** with the stated spend and conversions. The plugin has no step for sanity-checking client numbers.
- **The vertical playbook's solar section is thin** (renters, ACCC, CEC only). It says nothing on finance/credit claims or rebate-keyword intent.
