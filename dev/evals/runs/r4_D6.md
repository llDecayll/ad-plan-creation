# D6 Frames & Vows: Paid Ads Plan (Oct 2026). Quality gate run; no live checks done (Ad Library, Transparency Center, SERP, GTM/pixel, PageSpeed, Trends, policy updates all need a live pass).

## Real outcome, economics, transaction
- Real outcome: a net booking = token paid and not cancelled. Margin per package = 42% x ₹2.4L = ₹1,00,800 (assumed before ad spend). Expected margin per token = 72% x ₹1,00,800 = ₹72,576.
- Allowable CAC (3:1) = ₹33,600 per net booking; break-even = ₹1,00,800. Venue commission (8% x ₹6L = ₹48,000) is upside only. NCR travel ₹45,000 is treated as pass-through (confirm).
- Transaction: WhatsApp chat, quote, token, all off-platform. Meta/Google see only chat starts or clicks. Reconcile by manual tally (no CRM).
- History: ₹60k gave 190 chats (₹316 each) and 8 tokens (₹7,500 each; 4.2% of chats; n<20, so weak).

## Goal translation and declined asks
- "60 bookings by 31 Dec" is infeasible against 26 open team-dates. Retarget: fill 26 dates = 36 tokens at 28% cancellation, plus build a pipeline for 2027 dates. Couples book 8-14 months ahead, so Nov-Feb demand is thin. Owner to confirm open dates for Mar-Dec 2027 (sign-off: owner).
- "1,000+ weddings" is declined (actual 412). Use "412 weddings shot". Sign-off: owner.
- "5.0 rating" is declined. Use "4.6 from 38 Google reviews", plus a review drive. Sign-off: owner.
- Couples' and guests' photos: use only contracts with a usage clause or new written consent. Otherwise use detail shots, styled shoots or released couples; no identifiable guests. DPDP applies. Sign-off: owner plus contract review.
- "Official photographer of Aravali Palace" is declined (one wedding). Use "Shot at Aravali Palace, Udaipur" with the venue's written OK. Sign-off: owner.
- Brand reels as a goal are declined (capacity-bound). Reels are used as creative inside performance campaigns. Standalone awareness is deferred.
- Venue leads (sibling manager) are a separate goal and are deferred, not blended. Sign-off: owner and venue manager.

## Platform split
₹2.5L/mo = ₹8,200/day. Intent-order default is 55-70% Search. Shifted 15 points toward Meta because Meta's own history (₹7,500/token) beats the unproven Search estimate (₹13.5k). Of ₹7,400 deployed: Google 40.5%, Meta 59.5%. A 10% reserve (₹800) is released at day 14 to the winner.

## Operating constraints
- Move enquiries from the owner's personal WhatsApp to a WhatsApp Business number on the Page. Name the handler, reply within 15 minutes 9am-10pm, add an away message, and send a first-reply script that collects date, city and venue.
- The greeting carries a campaign code: "Hi, planning my wedding [FV-M1-JPR]".
- Tally sheet: chat, date open, quote, token, cancelled. CAPI lead marking weekly.
- Quote the NCR ₹45k travel up front.
- Q4 CPMs run +20-40% (Diwali; verify the date).
- The site needs a short enquiry form with a thank-you event for Google.

## Key risks

## Where the plugin was missing, unclear or contradictory (improvised)
- No wedding or photography vertical and no benchmark row. I used Local services and Events and derived the numbers (cost evidence 3).
- No rule for a goal exceeding inventory (26 dates), or for lead time (8-14 months) vs open near dates.
- "Token" vs "booking" and cancellation are undefined. I applied the 28% to the expected margin.
- 3:1 CAC: the margin base (before or after ad spend) is not stated.
- Test cap (10-20%) vs the 3x minimum conflicts; I used minimum-wins and kept tests at 19%.
- The 55-70% Search default vs history (<20 outcomes): I used the 15-point shift.
- Hard (J+U) plus soft (NCR) on a small budget: no Google rule, so I deferred it.
- Photo-consent for old contracts and use of a venue's name are not in policy-watch; the nearest row is "identifiable children".
- Quality gate F (5 creatives per campaign) vs K (word cap): I followed K.
- A stakeholder (venue manager) goal with unknown revenue ownership: the 15-25% cap was applied as a deferral.
