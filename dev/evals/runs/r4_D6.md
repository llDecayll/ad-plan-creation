## Real outcome, economics, transaction
- Real outcome: net booking = token paid and not cancelled. Margin per package 42% x ₹2.4L = ₹1,00,800 (assumed pre-ad-spend). Allowable CAC (3:1) = ₹33,600 per net booking; break-even ₹1,00,800. Venue commission (₹48,000) is upside only; NCR travel ₹45k treated as pass-through.

## Goal translation, declined asks
- "60 bookings by 31 Dec" is infeasible vs 26 open dates. Retarget: fill 26 dates = 36 tokens at 28% cancellation, plus a 2027 pipeline (couples book 8-14 months ahead; owner to confirm open 2027 dates). Sign-off: owner.
- "1,000+ weddings": declined; use "412 weddings".
- "5.0 rating": declined; use "4.6 from 38 Google reviews" plus a review drive.
- Couples'/guest photos: only contracts with a usage clause or new written consent; else detail shots or released couples; no identifiable guests (DPDP). Sign-off: owner plus contract review.
- "Official photographer of Aravali Palace": declined (one wedding); use "Shot at Aravali Palace" with venue written OK.
- Brand reels as the goal: declined (capacity-bound); reels used as creative.
- Venue leads (sibling manager): separate goal, deferred. Sign-off: owner and venue manager.
