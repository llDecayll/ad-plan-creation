# S2 VeloSkin: plan (r1)

## Real outcome and economics
- Real outcome: contribution margin on new-customer orders net of RTO. Transaction is on veloskin.in (Shopify), visible to Meta and Google (Purchase + value verified). RTO, delivery and repeat are invisible to platforms; reconcile weekly with Shopify and courier data.
- Break-even CPA = 900 x 70% = **₹630**; break-even ROAS **1.43**. 90-day value: 900 x 1.25 = ₹1,125 revenue, about ₹787 gross profit, so the 90-day CPA ceiling is about ₹790.
- Not supplied, so asked, not invented: shipping, COD share, RTO %, payment fees. ₹630 is the base figure, likely optimistic.
- Sanity check: ₹25k/day at mid CPA is about 40 orders/day, versus about 23/day today across all channels (2,100/90).

## Goal translation
"Online sales" is right; optimise to purchase value. **Declined:** the site claim "clears acne in 7 days" (cosmetics cannot claim to treat or cure; unsubstantiated). Alternative: ingredient-led, verifiable claims; remove before launch, since Merchant Center and Meta review read the landing page. No "your acne" phrasing.

## Platform split: Meta 64% (₹16,000), Google 36% (₹9,000)
Visual D2C starts at 60-75% Meta. No cost-per-outcome history, so intent order: Google captures existing search demand (feed approved), Meta creates demand; heavy competitor UGC supports Meta as the larger share.

## Risks / blockers
1. Acne claim (blocker). 2. Mid CPA sits at break-even. 3. COD/RTO/shipping unknown. 4. Spend is over 2x current order volume (absorption, fatigue). 5. Brand demand inflating PMax. 6. Meta/Google double counting.
