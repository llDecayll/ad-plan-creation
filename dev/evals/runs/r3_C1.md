# Taskloom (C1) - ad plan (USD; live checks not done, noted)

**Real outcome and economics.** Outcome: paying account. Gross-margin LTV = $24 x 80% / 6% churn = **$320** (not finance's $864, which has no churn or margin). At 8% trial-to-paid, break-even is **$320 per paid account = $25.60 per trial**. The ask ($40/trial) implies $500 per paid account: loses $180 each. Target CAC <= $215 (1.5x), ceiling $320. History: Google $11k/7 paid = $1,571; Meta $4k/2 = $2,000; LinkedIn $9k/0. **At current economics the $40k goal is unprofitable and unrealistic**; the plan buys proof, not volume.
**Transaction:** trial signup on app.taskloom.io (invisible to tags: no cross-domain), payment in Stripe (not sent). Reconcile via signup UTM/gclid capture and Stripe import.

**Goal translation.** "1,000 trials" becomes paid accounts at CAC <= $215-320. Growth lead (trials) vs CEO (paid): primary = activated trial (3 projects, 31%) feeding paid; raw-trial push capped at 15-25%; CEO signs off.
**Declined asks:** (1) "10x faster, #1 by freelancers": no study, unsubstantiated. Alternative: verifiable claims (5-minute setup, flat $24, annual = 2 months free), or commission a real benchmark. Sign-off: founder. (2) Asana trademark in headlines: complaint/disapproval risk (verify live). Alternative: bid competitor terms as keywords, headline "Simple project management alternative", factual comparison page. Sign-off: founder + counsel.

**Platform split.** History beats intent default: Google 65% / Meta 35% of launch spend. LinkedIn paused ($409/trial, 0 paid; 22 trials is thin).
