# A3 CareBridge Staffing (r1)

**Real outcome + economics.** Real outcome = a placed RN ($9,500 fee). Leading outcome = *qualified application*: completed Bullhorn application, active TX/compact licence, 1+ yr acute care, recruiter-verified. Value per qualified applicant = $9,500/6 = **$1,583** (gross; margin not supplied). Goal implies $18,000/200 = **$90** each, so break-even is ~17x higher and recruiter/hospital capacity binds before budget. Transaction happens in Bullhorn (external domain). Meta sees the instant-form lead natively plus a thank-you pixel; Google sees nothing. Reconciliation: weekly Bullhorn export by source/UTM plus recruiter tally.

**Goal translation.** "200 qualified RN applications" honoured, defined as above (not "leads").
Declined: women 24-40 only, and excluding over-50s (sex/age discrimination; blocked by the Employment category). Alternatives: all ages/genders; a "preferred shift" form question; night-differential copy; licence/experience knock-outs. "Within 10 miles of hospitals" is below the 15-mile category minimum, so overridden: Houston and Dallas pins, 15-25 mi.

**Platform split.** Meta 100% ($18,000); Google $0 funded (deferred). Meta has client history ($11/lead, 20% qualified = $55/qualified lead). Google has no history, no trackable conversion, CPC $4-9, and a ~$98/day minimum (15 clicks x ~$6.50) on blind Max-clicks. Derived Google cost per qualified application ~$70-550 (CPC / (3-8% apply x 55-70% qualified)), centre ~$190. Meta is cheaper per real outcome, so it gets the larger share.

*Budget check:* CPR $24 (2x the $11 history for knock-outs; US benchmark $8-40). Minimum 7 x 24 = $168; comfortable 10 x 24 = $240; $300 = 12.5x, passes. At downside CPR $36, 7 x 36 = $252, still passes. $2,100/wk / $24 = 87 leads/wk (>50). One ad set because two metro ad sets would need $336/day; read cities via breakdowns.

*Forecast (±35%):* $18,000/$24 = 750 leads; 45% qualified (history 20%, assumed lift) = 338 at $53; 60% complete Bullhorn (assumed) = **~203 qualified applications (132-274), $89 each ($66-136)**. Real outcome: 203/6 = **~34 placements (22-46)**, ~$530 per placement vs a $9,500 fee; most land after day 60. Chance of reaching 200: ~50%.

**Other channels:** Indeed/ZipRecruiter sponsored jobs (pay per application; likely best $ per RN), LinkedIn (travel nurses), email/SMS to the candidate database, referral bonuses, nursing job boards.

**Policy.** Employment category, US audience: **Required** (baseline 2026-10-04; live check not run). No age, gender, postcode or lookalikes; 15-mile minimum. India declaration n/a. Later Google: no age/gender/ZIP. Pay/bonus claims client-verified; no personal-attribute copy ("burned out?").

**Operating constraints.** Two recruiters, 48h screening is too slow: add instant auto-SMS with the Bullhorn link and a same-business-day call. ~12 leads/day x 15 min = ~3 h/day, OK; throttle if >20/day. Central time; always-on with a "call within 1 business day" confirmation.

**Risks.** fbclid/UTMs may not survive to Bullhorn (undercount; pixel "configured, firing unverified"); offer unknown; 48h SLA; client must accept declined targeting; hospital requisition capacity and margin unknown.
