# TrekNest (B6): Paid Ads Plan, October 2026

Assumed (ask): deposit = per booking; 22% margin before ad spend. No live checks done (site, GTM, Ad Library, SERP, policy); data is scenario or plugin baseline.

## Real outcome and economics
- Outcome: CRM-verified deposit that becomes a completed trek. Booking = ₹16,000 x 2.6 pax = ₹41,600; contribution at 22% = **₹9,152 per booking** (₹3,520 per person if deposits are per person).
- CRM cost per deposit (12 months): Meta ₹26L/1,940 = **₹1,340**; Google ₹14L/410 = **₹3,415** (2.5x dearer). Blended ₹40L/2,350 = ₹1,702.
- GA4 gives Google 61% of deposits; CRM says 410/2,350 = 17%. Brand clicks harvest Meta-created demand, so I trust the CRM.
- Transaction: enquiry on site/WhatsApp/phone, counsellor closes the deposit. Platforms see the form and CAPI deposit; reconcile in the CRM.

## Goal translation and declined ask
- Declined: "80% to Search" (₹3.2L/month, 2.7x current Google run-rate). Reason: CRM history shows Meta at 2.5x lower cost per deposit.
- 80/20 forecast: about 112-154 deposits/month vs 187-261 for this plan.
- Alternative: 71/29 plus a brand-search holdout to test "Search is intent", framed as the manager's hypothesis.
- Sign-off: marketing manager and the owner/founder (budget owner; assumed).

## Platform split and why
Meta ₹9,400/day (71%), Google ₹3,750/day (29%). History beats the intent default. Not 85%: Google needs about ₹3,415/day for smart bidding, brand defence, and Meta absorption is untested (+32% on about ₹7,100/day).
