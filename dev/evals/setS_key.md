# Expert answer key (written before any plugin output was seen)

Each checkpoint = what a strong senior performance marketer would do. Grade Pass / Partial / Fail.

## S1 SmileCraft Dental
1. Reframes "awareness" into appointment/lead goals (Leads objective on Meta; conversion goals on Google), with a stated reason (empty weekday chairs).
2. Uses Google Search for high-intent treatments (implants, aligners, root canal, "dentist near me") with call + location assets.
3. Meta campaign for consultation/offer-driven leads (WhatsApp or form), leveraging verified pixel/CAPI where sensible.
4. Budget split favours high-intent capture (Google ≥ ~40-50%) and keeps learning units few enough for ₹3,000/day.
5. Location: radius/area around the clinic, hard boundary settings (Presence on Google; living-in + expansion off on Meta) — notes HSR+Koramangala is small, watch reach.
6. Health policy: no personal-health targeting/assertions ("Do you have..."), careful with before/after, no guaranteed results.
7. Uses high-value services (implants, aligners) differently from low-value (cleaning) — e.g. separate ad groups/creatives or warns that ₹999-cleaning style offers attract low-value leads.
8. Mentions weekday-capacity tactics (ad scheduling/offers for weekday slots) or lead-quality tracking to bookings.
9. Confidence reasonably high (≈65-80) given verified tracking, fast replies, strong reviews.

2. Google Search on high-intent keywords is core; uses history (₹4,200/demo, 18% qualified ⇒ ~₹23k per qualified demo) to set expectations.
3. Recommends offline conversion import / optimising toward qualified demos from HubSpot (lead quality) rather than raw form fills.
4. De-prioritises or excludes Meta for prospecting (weak B2B targeting, no pixel), or limits it to retargeting.
5. Separate campaigns for India vs UAE (different CPCs, language/currency, competitors), each checked for budget sufficiency.

9. Confidence moderate-high for Google (has own data), lower for anything new.

## S4 Skyline Residences (Pune + NRI)
1. Identifies Meta housing special ad category: REQUIRED for the US audience; not required in India/UAE; splits campaigns by region.
2. States US-housing restrictions (no age/gender/postcode targeting, 15-mile min radius, no lookalikes) and adapts targeting.
3. Uses history: Meta forms cheap but low site-visit rate (3% ⇒ ~₹11,700 per visit) vs Google (12% ⇒ ~₹9,200 per visit) — compares on cost per site visit, not cost per lead.
4. Improves Meta lead quality: higher-intent forms, qualifying questions (budget, timeline, config, loan), conversion leads/CRM feedback (CAPI with site-visit event).
5. NRI considerations: time zones vs IST call-centre hours, WhatsApp/call-back scheduling, NRI-specific messaging (RERA, home loans for NRIs, video tours).
6. Budget allocation reflects quality data (more to Google or to quality-optimised Meta), each campaign above learning threshold.
7. RERA number in ads / real-estate compliance mention.
8. Google: Search on project/location/configuration keywords, possibly competitor project names, location option Presence vs interest for NRIs (NRIs abroad searching Pune property ⇒ interest-based targeting is legitimately useful here — a hard "presence" boundary on Pune would miss NRIs; plan must handle the conflict between "hard boundary" and NRI intent).
9. Confidence moderate-high with history.
