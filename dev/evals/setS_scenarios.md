Treat each block as the completed intake and research for that client. Do not browse.

## S1 — SmileCraft Dental (fictional), HSR Layout, Bengaluru
- Intake: website smilecraftdental.in; goal stated by client: "we want awareness in HSR and Koramangala"; budget ₹3,000/day total; location HSR Layout + Koramangala, hard boundary; Facebook + Instagram exist; no placement preference.
- Who answers: reception answers phone and WhatsApp 9am-8pm, usually within 10 minutes.
- Business: 2-chair clinic; services: implants (₹35k+), clear aligners (₹60k+), root canal, cleaning, kids' dentistry. Clinic owner privately says "honestly we need more patients booking, chairs are empty on weekdays".
- Website: fast mobile page, booking form on /book, WhatsApp button, Google rating 4.8 (310 reviews). Offer: free consultation + X-ray worth ₹500.
- Tracking: Meta Pixel + Conversions API verified firing (Lead on form submit, Contact on WhatsApp click); Google Ads conversion on booking form verified; GA4 linked.
- Competitors: 6 dental clinics in HSR/Koramangala on Meta (aligner and implant offers, "₹999 cleaning", running 30-120 days); 4 clinics bidding on "dentist near me", "dental implants HSR" on Google.
- History: none.

## S2 — VeloSkin (fictional), D2C organic skincare, ships all India

- Website claims "clears acne in 7 days" on the serum page.
