# LinkedIn and X Ads playbook (planned channels)

Menus and limits change often: verify live before launch. Numbers below are **derived planning ranges**, label them "derived" (cost evidence ≤3, bands ±50%). Same guardrails apply: no ad-account access, aggregate data only.

## LinkedIn Campaign Manager
- **Objectives:** Brand awareness, Website visits, Engagement, Video views, Lead generation, Website conversions, Job applicants (names vary).
- **When it earns budget:** B2B or high-ticket services where one customer is worth many times the cost per lead; target by job title, seniority, industry, company size, company lists (ABM) and Matched Audiences (website retargeting, contact lists with documented consent).
- **Formats:** single image, video, document (carousel PDFs), thought-leader ads (a person's post), conversation/message ads (watch frequency), Lead Gen Forms (pre-filled; higher volume, lower intent: add a qualifying question).
- **Costs (derived, India vs global):** CPC about ₹250-700 India, $5-12 US/UK; cost per lead about ₹2,500-7,000 India, $60-150+ US; audience should be ≥~50,000 (smaller needs ABM lists and low frequency). Check minimum daily budget live.
- **Rules:** one offer per campaign; send leads to a CRM tally; qualified rate matters more than CPL. Optimise to the lead event, use Lead Gen Forms when mobile and speed matter. Judge on qualified leads and pipeline, not impressions.
- **Policy:** no discrimination in targeting by protected attributes; no personal-attribute copy; finance/health/jobs rules; verify live.

## X Ads
- **Objectives:** Reach, Video views, Website traffic, Engagement, Followers, App installs, Conversions (website) (names vary).
- **When it earns budget:** launches, events, news-driven or conversation-driven categories, tech/finance/media/gaming/sports audiences, and creator or customer-support-heavy brands. Weak as the only lower-funnel channel in most local-service plans.
- **Targeting:** keywords in conversations, follower look-alikes, interests, events, custom audiences (lists, website visitors with the X pixel). Use brand-safety exclusions.
- **Formats:** video (short, captioned, sound-off readable), image, text with link, carousel, amplify pre-roll on publisher video.
- **Costs (derived):** CPM about ₹80-250 India (varies widely), CPC ₹10-60, cost per website conversion often high: run as a **test line** with a 14-day read and kill rule.
- **Policy:** political and sensitive-event content restrictions; check live; no ads next to content you would not accept (brand safety).

## Roles in the plan
- LinkedIn: ABM/B2B lead gen and retargeting of site visitors; X: awareness/conversation and event/launch spikes; both feed website retargeting pools and Google brand Search.
- Both get: flight dates, targets with alert rules, a `monitor` block, ≥5 creative angles if funded (2 if planned), and a day-3/7/14/30 test-and-scale row.
