# Lead form question library

Applies to Meta instant forms and Google lead form assets. Fewer fields = more leads; more qualifying questions = better leads. Pick by the goal: volume (2-3 fields) or quality (add 2-3 qualifying questions + higher-intent review step).

## Always
- Contact: full name, phone (pre-filled). Email only if the sales process uses email.
- Intro screen: offer + what happens next ("We'll WhatsApp you within 30 minutes").
- Privacy: link to the client's privacy policy; one line on purpose of use (DPDP in India, GDPR in UK).
- Thank-you screen: next step + button (WhatsApp, call, or website).
- Meta form type: "More volume" for cheap tests; "Higher intent" (review screen) when lead quality matters.
- Prefer multiple-choice questions over short answer (faster, cleaner data). Use conditional logic where available.

## By vertical (choose 2-3)
**Home services (painting, interiors, renovation)**
- Type of work: Interior / Exterior / Both / Waterproofing / Texture
- Property: Apartment / Independent house / Villa / Office
- Size: 1 BHK / 2 BHK / 3 BHK / 4+ BHK / Not sure
- When do you want to start? This week / This month / 1-3 months / Just exploring
- Area / locality (or pin code) — to filter out-of-area leads

**Dental / clinics / physio**
- Which treatment are you interested in? (list services, no condition questions)
- Preferred time: Morning / Afternoon / Evening / Weekend
- Preferred branch (if multiple)
- How soon? This week / This month / Exploring
(Do not ask about medical conditions or symptoms — policy and privacy.)

**Real estate**
- Budget band (ranges)
- Configuration: 2 BHK / 3 BHK / Plot / Villa
- Purpose: Own use / Investment
- Timeline to buy: 0-3 / 3-6 / 6-12 months / Exploring
- Home loan needed? Yes / No / Not sure
(Housing special ad category on Meta: targeting is restricted; the form does the qualifying.)

**Farmland / investment products**
- Investment range (bands)
- Timeline
- Have you invested in land before? Yes / No
(Avoid return promises in form copy. Securities/investment declaration may apply.)

**Solar**
- Property type: Independent house / Apartment society / Commercial
- Monthly electricity bill band
- Roof ownership: Own / Rented
- Timeline

**Education**
- Course of interest
- Current status: Student / Working / Other
- Start: This intake / Next intake / Exploring

**B2B services**
- Company size band
- Role
- What are you looking for? (service list)
- Timeline / budget band

## Disqualifier trick
Include one question that lets low-intent people self-select out (e.g. "Just exploring" timeline). Tag those leads low-priority in the CRM rather than rejecting them.

## WhatsApp quick replies (click-to-WhatsApp)
2-4 tap options that pre-qualify, e.g. "Interior", "Exterior", "Both", "Get a price range".
