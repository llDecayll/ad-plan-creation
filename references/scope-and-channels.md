# Scope, markets and off-platform channels

A senior planner starts from where the money is made and accounts for everything the client asked for.

## 1. Where does the transaction actually happen? (mandatory check)
Before choosing platforms, list where customers convert and which of those places Meta/Google can see.
| If conversion happens on… | Implication |
|---|---|
| Own website / app with tracking | Platform optimisation possible |
| WhatsApp / calls / instant forms | Platform counts the conversation/lead; downstream outcomes need CRM tagging or offline upload |
| Aggregators (Swiggy, Zomato, Uber Eats, Talabat, Deliveroo) | Platform can't see orders. Recommend aggregator in-app ads as a channel; Meta/Google traffic there is a weak, untracked option |
| Marketplaces (Amazon, Flipkart, Noon, Nykaa, Myntra) | Marketplace ads (Sponsored Products) usually beat sending traffic; brand-store traffic needs attribution tags (e.g. Amazon Attribution) |
| Portals (99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com, Bayut, Property Finder, Rightmove, Practo, Justdial, Sulekha, UrbanCompany) | Often a meaningful share of leads; recommend budget share or listing upgrades |
| Third-party ticketing / ATS / application portals | Tag access often limited; use UTMs + post-purchase/post-application confirmation pixels where allowed, or optimise to the last trackable step and reconcile |
| Physical store / walk-in | Store visits, Google Business Profile, local inventory/location assets, offline conversion import, offer codes |

## 2. Off-platform channel check (always include, even if out of scope)
The plugin plans Meta and Google. When another channel is a better fit, say so in a short "Other channels to consider" block with a rough budget share and why. Typical:
- **B2B by job title / company size:** LinkedIn (lead gen forms, ABM lists). Often 30-50% of a B2B budget.
- **Local businesses:** Google Business Profile (free; reviews, posts, maps), Local Services Ads where available, Practo/Justdial-type directories.
- **Food delivery:** aggregator ads. **Retail D2C:** marketplace ads, influencer/creator partnerships (Meta partnership ads, whitelisting).
- **Real estate:** property portals; channel partners/brokers. **Apps:** Apple Search Ads, Google App campaigns, Meta app campaigns.
- **Youth/entertainment:** TikTok (where legal), Snapchat, YouTube Shorts. **Nonprofits:** Google Ad Grants, Meta fundraisers.
- **Retention:** email/WhatsApp broadcast to the client's own list (often cheapest revenue).
Mark these "outside this plugin — plan separately".

## 3. Every requested market or audience is accounted for
For each location/audience the client named (e.g. UAE, NRIs in US, a second city):
- Either a campaign (with its own budget check), or a line under **Deferred** stating: why, the ₹ needed per day to run it properly, the expected cost per outcome range, and "client to decide". Never silently drop or merge it.
- Separate campaigns per country when currency, language, CPCs, policy (special ad categories, consent) or time zones differ. If the budget can't support separate campaigns, prefer funding one market properly and deferring the other over merging markets with different rules — merging is allowed only when policy and language are identical and you state the trade-off.

## 4. Diaspora / NRI / expat audiences
- Meta: target the countries where they live ("living in"), use language/creative to self-select (e.g. "for NRIs in Dubai"); Advantage+ suggestions such as "Expats (from India)" where available. Housing/employment/credit categories apply by the audience's country (US/Canada/Europe).
- Google: a separate campaign targeting the overseas countries with **Presence** plus geo-intent keywords ("flats in Pune for NRI", "<project> Pune"), OR the home-city campaign with **Presence or interest** to catch people abroad searching for that city. A "hard boundary" on the home city alone would miss them — call out the conflict and choose deliberately.
- Operations: time zones vs call-centre hours (scheduled callbacks, WhatsApp, video tours), NRI-specific objections (home loans for NRIs, power of attorney, repatriation, RERA/regulator assurance).

## 5. Conflicting stakeholder goals
When two decision-makers want different things (e.g. chairman wants awareness, admissions head wants applications; franchisor vs franchisee), name the conflict, recommend which goal gets which budget and why, and give each its own campaign and success metric. Don't blend objectives in one campaign.
