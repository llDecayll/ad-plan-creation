# Plan output template

This is the content checklist. The default format is the HTML journey board in `html-output.md`; the mapping table there says where each section below goes. Use this section order as-is only for Word/Markdown output, when the user asks for it.

Title: `<Client> — Paid Ads Plan — <Month YYYY>`
Subtitle: Prepared by Iugale Services Pvt Ltd · <date> · Overall confidence <score>/100 (<band>)

Write for a busy reader: decisions first, evidence after. Tables where comparing; short prose elsewhere.

## 1. Executive summary (one page max)
- The goal as understood, translated into platform objectives.
- The recommendation in 3-5 lines: platforms, number of campaigns, conversion locations, budget split.
- Forecast range for the first 30 days (results, cost per result, and qualified/closed estimate if possible).
- Overall confidence score and band.
- Top 3 blockers or fixes before launch.
- Assumptions made (if any intake answers were missing).

## 2. Confidence summary table
Item | Score | Band | Main reason | What would raise it

## 3. Research findings
3.1 Website audit · 3.2 Tracking audit table · 3.3 Competitor landscape (table: competitor, Meta ads?, Google ads?, offer, formats, longest-running ad, takeaway) · 3.4 Market & seasonality · 3.5 Audience psychology (fear, desire, objection, decision-maker, decision time)
Mark each finding "verified live <date>" or "benchmark/playbook".

## 4. Strategy
- Platform split and why (and why not the alternative).
- Funnel map: which campaign does which job.
- Budget allocation table: campaign, platform, daily budget, expected cost per result, expected results/week, passes learning threshold? (Yes/No).
- What we deliberately are NOT doing yet, and the trigger to add it.

## 5. Meta plan (if included)
From meta-ads-planner: campaign-by-campaign settings tables, audiences, placements, instant form or WhatsApp setup, exclusions, launch checklist.

## 6. Google plan (if included)
From google-ads-planner: campaign-by-campaign settings, keyword themes and negatives, RSA copy, assets, conversion actions, bidding progression, launch checklist.

## 7. Creative prompts
Per campaign, the five (or more) prompts in the framework format.

## 8. Lead forms & WhatsApp scripts
Form questions, intro/thank-you text, WhatsApp greeting and quick replies, first-reply script for whoever answers.

## 9. Tracking & URL parameters
Fixes needed, exact URL parameter strings, CRM fields to capture.

## 10. Policy & risks
From policy-watch.md, with dates checked.

## 10b. Feasibility, channel roles and flights
Goal feasibility verdict and funnel map · Channel-role plan table (omnipresence.md) · Flight table per campaign (start, end, length, phases, end action) · Targets and alert rules per campaign.

## 11. Launch & optimisation timeline
Day 0 checklist · Days 1-3 (don't touch) · Days 4-7 (first read) · Week 2 (creative refresh) · Weeks 3-4 (one test at a time) · Monthly lead-quality review.
Include the "send me these numbers on day 7" list for the results review, and the monitoring cadence (day 3/7/14/21/30, then weekly) for `ad-monitor`.

## 12. Appendix
Confidence factor breakdowns, benchmark sources, full competitor notes.
