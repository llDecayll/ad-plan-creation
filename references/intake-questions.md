# Intake questions

Ask once, in one round. Skip anything already answered. Keep wording plain.

## Required
1. **Website URL.** Also ask for the specific landing page if ads should send people somewhere other than the home page.
2. **Business goal, in business terms.** Example prompts: "How many enquiries/sales a month, at roughly what cost each?" "Is this a launch, a seasonal push, or always-on?" Accept "awareness / traffic / leads / engagement / sales / app installs" if that's how the user answers, but translate it in Phase C.
3. **Facebook Page URL and Instagram URL.** If not given, try to find them on the website footer/header first; ask only if not found.
4. **Location.**
   - Where to target: country, state, city, radius around an address, or specific areas/pin codes.
   - **Hard or soft boundary?**
     - Hard = ads only to people who live in / are in that area, with expansion options turned off.
     - Soft = the platform may also show ads to people interested in or recently in the area.
5. **Daily or monthly budget** and currency. Ask whether it covers Meta and Google together or each separately.

## Ask as a preference, not a requirement
6. **Placements.** "Any surfaces you want to avoid (e.g. Audience Network, Display partners, YouTube)?" Default recommendation is automatic placements with justified exclusions.
7. **Who handles enquiries, and how fast?** (Phone, WhatsApp, CRM, hours.) This decides the conversion location.
8. **Margin / unit economics.** Gross margin or contribution per sale, AOV or deal value, close rate, if known.
8b. **Offer.** Any discount, free consultation, free site visit, limited-time deal? If none, the plan will suggest one.
9. **Past results (optional).** A campaign-level export (CSV or screenshots) and outcome counts by campaign: leads, qualified, appointments/visits, closed. No individual lead details.
10. **Learnings ledger (optional).** If they have the ad-learnings ledger file from previous runs, attach it.

## Do NOT ask
- Conversion location, campaign types, objectives, bid strategies, audiences: recommend these.
- Anything visible on the website.

## Hard vs soft boundary: settings this implies
| | Hard boundary | Soft boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Meta location type | "People living in this location" when the customer must reside there (home services, real estate, admissions, local recruitment); "living in or recently in" when presence is enough (restaurants, clinics, gyms, events near offices) | People living in or recently in (default) |
| Meta "Reach more people likely to respond" (location expansion) | Off (unticked) | On |
| Meta Advantage+ audience | Keep on for age/interest, but location stays a hard limit | On |
| Google location option | Presence: people in or regularly in your targeted locations | Presence or interest |
| Google excluded locations | Add surrounding areas you cannot serve if needed | Not needed |
