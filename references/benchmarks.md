## Missing benchmark protocol
1. Live search for a current benchmark for the vertical + country (prefer platform/industry reports from the last 12 months).
2. If none, derive from a related row and a market multiplier, widen the range to ±50%, label it "derived", and score cost evidence 3.
3. Never invent a precise number; always show the derivation.

## Learning thresholds (platform guidance, approximate)
- Meta: ~50 optimisation events per ad set within ~7 days to exit learning. Budget changes over ~20%, new creative, audience edits and long pauses can reset it.
- Google smart bidding: ~30+ conversions in 30 days per campaign for target CPA to be stable; start with Maximise conversions (or Maximise clicks if no tracking yet, with a cap).

## Budget rule
Use references/budget-allocation.md §1 (Meta ≥ 7 × CPR per ad set; Google ≥ 1 × CPA and ≥ 15 clicks/day).

### Reach and awareness (India, Meta/YouTube, 2026 baseline, ±50%)
Meta reach CPM ₹150-300; YouTube bumper/skippable CPM ₹120-250; CPV ₹0.5-2. Target weekly frequency 2-4. Reach ≈ budget / CPM x 1,000 / frequency. Festive weeks (Diwali, IPL) add 20-40% to CPM.

### Funnel conversion anchors (use only when the client has none; label "assumed")
Conversation → qualified lead 25-40%; lead → booked visit/appointment 30-50%; booked → attended 50-70%; conversation → paid order (food/D2C via WhatsApp) 10-25%; ticket page visit → purchase 2-6%.
