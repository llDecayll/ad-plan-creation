# Monitor brief (the prompt you explain to the monitor)

Copy this, fill it in plain words, and attach it with each export. The monitor turns your words into rules, and tells you where it disagrees with the plan.

```
Client / campaign(s):
Goal in one line:
What counts as a good result (number and unit):
When to worry (cost, volume, quality, frequency):
What I am willing to change on my own (budget up to X%, creative, audience):
What needs approval (location, offer, objective):
Hard limits (maximum spend, locations, claims we must not make):
Things you may not know (festival, stock-out, staff on leave, price change, new competitor):
Anything I tested or edited this week:
What I want from this check (health read / change prompts / next-campaign ideas / client report):
```

Examples of rules in plain words:
- "If cost per lead stays above ₹300 for 3 days, tell me to fix or pause."
- "Never go above ₹1,000/day without asking."
- "Only Karnataka. If the data shows other states, flag it."
- "Do not run discounts."
The monitor stores accepted rules in the plan's `monitor` block so the next check uses them.
