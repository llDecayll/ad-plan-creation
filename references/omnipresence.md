# Omnipresence: one plan across Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Google/YouTube and the website

Being everywhere helps only when the channels work as one system. It does not mean equal spend, or the same ad everywhere. Use this file in Phase C of ad-strategy to write the **Channel-role plan**.

## 1. Channel roles (give every channel a job)
| Channel | Best job in the funnel | Typical conversion location | Qualify it when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Search | Capture existing intent (lower funnel) | Website, call, WhatsApp | Real search demand exists |
| YouTube / Demand Gen | Video reach and consideration; feeds brand search | Website, brand search lift | Video assets exist; awareness matters |
| Facebook + Instagram (Meta) | Create demand, retarget, WhatsApp/lead forms | WhatsApp, instant form, website | Consumer or local audience; visual offer |
| LinkedIn | B2B targeting by job title, company, industry; ABM | Lead Gen Form, website | B2B, high ticket (value per customer well above LinkedIn cost per lead) |
| X | Conversation, news, launches, tech/finance/media audiences, real-time events | Website, followers/engagement (rarely the main converter) | Audience is active on X and brand-safe content exists |
| Website | The hub: converts, owns the tracking, builds the retargeting pools for every platform | Form, WhatsApp, call, checkout | Always |
| Owned (email, WhatsApp broadcast, organic social) | Retention and repeat; cheapest outcome | WhatsApp, email | Always when a list exists |

Facebook and Instagram are one Meta campaign structure (placements), not two channels: plan them together and read results by placement.

## 2. How the channels support each other
- **Upper funnel feeds lower funnel:** video views (Meta/YouTube/X/LinkedIn) build retargeting pools and lift brand searches; read the lift in Google brand Search and direct traffic.
- **Intent catches the demand others created:** keep brand Search funded in proportion (budget-allocation.md); expect last-click to credit Search and understate Meta/YouTube/X. Judge by total cost per real outcome (MER, CRM), with a brand-search holdout test when stakeholders disagree.
- **One message, native formats:** same offer and proof across channels, adapted to each channel's format and tone (Reels 9:16 hook, LinkedIn document or founder video, X short video or text with a link, YouTube 6s/15s).
- **Sequence, do not shout:** cold → warm → hot. Cap overlap: when the same people can be hit on 3+ platforms, set frequency caps per platform (Meta ≤3/week; YouTube ≤3/week reach; LinkedIn ≤1-2/week for small ABM lists) and exclude converters everywhere.
- **Shared measurement:** one UTM scheme (url-parameters.md) with the same campaign code on every channel; weekly reconciliation against CRM/backend.

## 3. Budget and qualification rules (derived; verify live)
- Fund channels in order of cost per real outcome. A channel is **qualified** only if: the audience is present, the budget meets its minimum learning unit (budget-allocation.md §1), tracking is ready (or a labelled test), and there is creative capacity.
- Indicative minimum meaningful monthly budget (derived, ±50%): Meta ₹30-45k, Google Search ₹30-45k, YouTube ₹30-60k, LinkedIn ₹60-90k (CPCs high, B2B only), X ₹30-60k as a **test line** (≤10-15% of budget) unless the audience is X-native. Below the minimum, do not spread thin: concentrate on two channels and list others under Deferred with a trigger.
- Typical starting shares (adjust to history): local services 60-70% Google+Meta, remainder owned/other; D2C 60-75% Meta; B2B 30-50% LinkedIn + Search; awareness launches Meta/YouTube heavy. LinkedIn and X shares are **derived, not benchmarked**.
- Never run a channel only because it "completes the set": no audience, no budget, no tracking = deferred.

## 4. Output: the Channel-role plan (always include)
A table in the plan (and a Summary-tab section): channel | role | stage | campaign(s) | share of budget | flight | why now / trigger | key risk. Plus one paragraph: how the channels support each other for this client, the frequency caps, and how the combined effect will be measured (MER, brand-search lift, holdout).
Planned LinkedIn/X flows appear on the board as `planned` with a trigger and flight length; funded ones follow `linkedin-x-playbook.md`.
