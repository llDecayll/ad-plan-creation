# Claims and compliance quick rules (read with policy-watch.md)

Always state the decision, the compliant alternative and the sign-off person. "Verify live" for current platform wording.

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Competitor trademarks | Do not use competitor brand names in headlines/copy; bid on them as keywords only where policy allows; factual comparison pages need substantiation. |
| Superlatives and comparisons | "#1", "10x faster", "cheaper than X", "lowest price guaranteed": need evidence; hotel/OTA parity clauses can prohibit best-rate claims. |
| Hygiene / ratings | Only per-outlet facts that are true today. |
| Financial advice (SEBI/ICAI/FCA etc.) | No assured returns, testimonials with returns, or "No. 1" claims; compliance sign-off; licence number in ads. |
| Health and fertility | No guarantees; success rates only with definition, cohort and year; no sex-selection ads (illegal in India, PCPNDT); no personal-attribute copy; ART/ICMR rules. |
| Health data | Never send diagnoses, appointment types or portal URLs to Meta/Google tags; strip URLs; remove tags from portals; build audiences from public pages only. |
| Sanctions, export controls, weapons | Decline sanctioned countries and ammunition/ordnance; add negatives; keep from ad domain; export-compliance sign-off. |
| List uploads | Upload customer lists only with documented consent (GDPR/DPDP); EU trade-show leads need opt-in. |
| Subscription pricing | Show trial terms, renewal and cancellation. |
| Alcohol, energy drinks, gambling-adjacent | See guardrails.md §7 and vertical-playbooks.md. |
